# What a strategy's action is checked for

[What a strategy asks for](the-roads-around-the-checks.md) is one record of thirteen fields, and each is weighed on its own before the kind of action is considered at all.

## The one field required

The kind of action. It must be one of the four named kinds, and nothing stands in front of that test.

## The twelve that may be absent

Every other field has its test standing behind a test for its absence. So leaving a field out is never refused here — what a given kind of action needs is settled later.

## Five kinds

The direction, the way of working, the kind of order and the maker preference must each be one of their own named kinds. A bare word carrying the right spelling fails, the test asking what the value is rather than what it says.

## Three figures

The size, the amount of money to spend and the reference price must each be a finite figure of the exact kind standing above nought.

## One count

The deadline must be a whole number above nought. A true-or-false is refused by a test standing beside the one for kind, which is there because a true-or-false answers as a whole number.

## Two references

The trade's and the command's references must each be text with something left once trimmed. They are tested trimmed and carried as they arrived.

## Then the kind

Only once all thirteen have been weighed are [the requirements of the kind](what-each-kind-of-action-requires.md) looked at. So a bad figure is reported before a missing field.

The thirteen carry fourteen refusals between them: [the fields a change rewrites](the-two-mappings-an-action-reseals.md) are refused both for not being a mapping and for being an empty one.

# What the mark sampler is built with

[The sampler](the-mark-sampler.md) is built with an account, a pair, a way of reading the mark, a way of recording a sample, a clock and an interval. Three of those are checked.

## The two names

Each is tested for its truth and nothing more. An empty name is refused; a name of blank space alone is kept as it stands, and goes on every sample the sampler records.

That is the same escape [the shared check Praxis names its records by](the-escape-in-the-shared-check-for-a-name.md) leaves open, in a third place on this side.

## The interval

Refused at or below nought, by comparison. Nothing is asked of its kind, and two kinds get through.

An interval that is not a number is kept, and the wait between samples then ends immediately, so the loop samples over and over with no pause at all.

An interval given as an exact figure — the kind Praxis counts money in — is kept as well, and the wait raises on it. That raise is outside the catch that covers the sampling itself, so the loop's task ends with it, and the sampler reports itself stopped while nothing has been recorded but the first sample.

The register has found six records on these two sides refusing a figure by comparing it rather than asking what it is. This is the sixth, and the first where what is kept ends the work.

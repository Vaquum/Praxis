# When protection in doubt is remediated rather than waited on

A bracket whose protection the venue will not confirm waits, and the wait ends at a deadline measured from the moment the doubt began.

## The wait

A bracket carrying no moment the doubt began at is left alone — there is nothing to measure from, and nothing here sets one.

The wait is measured from that moment against the restore deadline. A wait short of it ends the pass, so the bracket is looked at again and again until the deadline passes or [the asking](how-a-protection-in-doubt-is-re-confirmed.md) settles it.

## What is remediated

What is left open is the entry's delivered figure less what the closing command has filled. Nothing left open means nothing done.

Otherwise the bracket is remediated on those figures, with every name the pair could still rest under handed over so the close can ask after each before it places anything.

## Whose figures they are

Both are the record's own. The venue's count of the pair is read elsewhere, by a routine that asks the venue leg by leg — and this road is the road taken when that asking kept failing, so the record's figures are used because the venue's cannot be had.

# What a failed protection replacement is looked for

A send of the replacement protective pair that fails is not taken at its word: the venue is asked whether the pair is resting after all, and the answer decides which of three endings follows.

## The question

A send that fails for any of three reasons — the send timing out, the venue refusing the name as a duplicate, or any other venue failure — is followed by asking after the pair by the name it was sent under.

## The three answers

An asking that cannot be answered leaves the standing unknown, and remembers the replacement's name as one still owing, so the watch knows to ask about it.

An answer saying the pair is nowhere has the bracket closed out as unprotected, with the old pair's name and the replacement's both handed over as candidates to ask after first.

An answer finding the pair is treated as the send's own: the pair is recorded under the venue's list name and the legs it reports, and the change carries on exactly as a send that answered would.

## What the found pair is missing

Fills given at placement. That road reports none, where the road of a send that answered carries whatever the venue gave — so a pair found by asking contributes nothing to the closing side's figures at this point, whatever it filled on the way up.

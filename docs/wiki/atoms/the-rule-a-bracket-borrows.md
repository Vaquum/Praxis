# The rule a bracket borrows

[The record of a bracket beginning](protection.md) carries the two protective legs as flat fields, and gets its rule about them from somewhere else.

## The build that is thrown away

As it is made, it builds [a protective-settings record](the-settings-each-way-of-working-takes.md) out of five of its own fields. The built thing is bound to no name, so nothing is kept of it.

## What the build is for

The settings record checks itself as it is made. Each leg must carry exactly one of an outright price and a distance, and each of the five figures that is given must be a positive finite figure, with any left out passed over.

Building one and discarding it is how those checks come to be run on the event's fields.

## Why it reads oddly

The event holds no test that weighs one of its fields against another, yet two of its fields are weighed against each other. A reader looking for the rule in the event finds a build with no result and no name.

The flat fields are what travels: the settings record exists for the length of the check and the event keeps the fields it was built from.

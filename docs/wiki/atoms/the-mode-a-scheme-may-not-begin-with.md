# The mode a scheme may not begin with

The record of [a scheme beginning](work-fed-out-over-time.md) names the way of working the scheme is to use, and asks one thing of it: that it not be the single-shot one.

## Asked by exclusion

The test compares the field with that one name and refuses a match. Every other way of working passes, and nothing anywhere in the record lists which ones are meant.

## What that lets through

Because the field is weighed by comparison with one name rather than by being asked what it is, a value that is no way of working at all passes. A word, a number, or nothing at all goes through, none of them being the name refused.

## The other way round

[The same question elsewhere](what-the-shared-checks-pass-over.md) is asked by inclusion: a field is matched against a frozen set of the named values, or against a kind. Those tests name what is allowed, so anything outside it is refused.

Here one value is named and the rest allowed. A way of working added later would be accepted by this record without anything being changed, and so would a mistake.

# Which accounts the figures road knows

The figures road serves what a paper run has made for one account, and which accounts it will answer for are the ones the settings name rather than the ones that are running.

## What it asks of the caller

A loopback address, or the caller is refused as forbidden. That is one of the three things [an operator road](what-every-operator-road-weighs-first.md) demands, and the only one demanded here — no token is needed.

Where the host has no record of its own, the road answers that it is not ready.

## Where its accounts come from

A mapping built once from the settings and kept, so the settings are read from disk on the first ask and never again. Each account in it carries the one standing symbol, the pool from its settings, and [the series and width its settings settle](how-the-mark-price-series-is-settled.md).

Where no account is named and exactly one is configured, that one is used. Anything else unnamed, or a name that is not among them, is refused as unknown with the known ones listed.

## The two different resolvings

This mapping is over the accounts the settings name. [The operator roads](what-every-operator-road-weighs-first.md) resolve over the accounts whose runtimes are published — the ones that have finished building.

So the figures road will answer for an account that has not started, and the operator roads will not. The same name reaches two different answers depending on which road it arrives on.

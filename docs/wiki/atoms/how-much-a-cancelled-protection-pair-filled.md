# How much a cancelled protection pair filled

The figure a replacement protective pair is sized against is the venue's own count of what the cancelled pair filled, read leg by leg.

## The reading

The list is asked for by its name, each leg it names is asked after in turn, and the legs' figures are summed. The sum is the answer.

## What a failure does

Nothing here catches a venue failure, so one reaches the caller — which turns it into the pair's standing becoming unknown, with the change halted for the watch rather than carried on.

So the figure is either the venue's, in full, or there is no figure and no change.

## The same questions asked twice

[The judging of whether a leg is still working](how-a-cancelled-protection-pair-is-judged-still-working.md) asks the venue exactly these two questions — the list by name, then each leg — and treats a failure quite differently: it answers that the pair is still working rather than letting the failure out.

What each answer is for differs. This figure goes on to size what replaces the pair. That one decides whether a close may go out at all, and the answer a failure gives there is the one that stops it.

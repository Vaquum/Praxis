# What the capital stage refuses

[The stage that sets money aside](how-nexus-sets-money-aside.md) refuses one thing of its own before it reaches the money: a life-span for the holding that is not a whole number above nought.

## The life-span

A whole number, above nought. A true-or-false is refused outright by a test of its own, which it needs: the test beside it counts a true-or-false as a whole number.

A life-span may be left out. Left out, nothing is passed on and the controller's own span stands — so the stage either names a span or says nothing about one.

## What the denial says

Where the money is not granted, the stage reports what the controller gave as its reason. A reason that is empty, or only blank space, is replaced with a standing message, so a refusal always carries words.

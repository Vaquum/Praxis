# The builder that stands apart

[A mode's settings](the-payload-a-modes-settings-arrive-in.md) have their record chosen from a table of one per way of working — with one exception, answered before the table is consulted.

## The exception

The plain single order. Its settings go to a builder of their own, which is where [the payload it may leave out](the-payload-a-modes-settings-arrive-in.md) is answered.

## What the two roads share

Either hands back a settings object that is already of the right kind, unexamined. Neither looks inside such an object.

Where a mapping arrives instead, the two part: the plain single order's builder tests each value as it reads it, and the table's road fills the record and leaves the testing to the record's own building.

So a reader asking what refuses a bad figure gets a different answer by way of working, though both answers end in a refusal.

## The change's side

A change's settings have no exception. Their record is chosen from a table of its own, for every way of working alike, so the plain single order's change goes the same road as a ladder's.

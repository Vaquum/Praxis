# What the platform's ceilings must be

[The stage weighing the platform's ceilings](what-the-platform-ceilings-weigh.md) is handed two records: the ceilings, and the figures to weigh against them. Each checks its own fields the same way.

## The walk each record makes

Every field may be left out. One that is given must be a figure of the exact kind, finite, and at or above nought — so a ceiling of nought is allowed, and a figure below nought is not.

The ceilings walk five fields; the record of figures walks four.

## The share's own ceiling

One field in each record is a share of something rather than an amount. Each is held to a further test, run after the walk: at or below one.

So a finite share above one passes the walk and is turned back by the test that follows. A share without end never gets that far, the walk asking for finiteness first.

## How the stage answers

A refusal found while weighing is kept rather than answered with there and then. The allowance is given only where the weighing kept none.

# How Praxis and Nexus work

Praxis sends orders to a venue and keeps the record of what happened. Nexus
decides what to send. One process runs both, and these short articles explain
one piece each, in plain words.

New here? Read [Trades, requests and orders](atoms/what-a-trade-is.md), then
[What an account is](atoms/what-an-account-is.md),
[Order placement](atoms/how-an-order-is-placed.md),
[The account worker](atoms/how-waiting-work-is-drained.md) and
[Outcomes](atoms/what-an-outcome-is.md). After that, open the group holding your
question. Every term is explained in the article that owns it, and linked from
the others where it appears.

## Orders, accounts and the venue

- [Trades, requests and orders](atoms/what-a-trade-is.md) — a trade, a request and an order: work from outside, one piece of it, and one send to the venue.
- [What an account is](atoms/what-an-account-is.md) — a name that groups the requests, orders, holdings and queues kept about it.
- [What a venue is](atoms/what-a-venue-is.md) — whatever Praxis sends orders to: one per host, called by name, so a live exchange and a simulator run the same code.
- [What a venue must offer](atoms/what-a-venue-must-offer.md) — Praxis writes down what it needs of any exchange as a list of named things with no code behind them, so the file says what must be answered to and nothing about how.
- [What an execution report is held to](atoms/what-an-execution-report-is-held-to.md) — a report that claims a trade must carry the numbers proving it, and a report of anything else has those numbers taken away.
- [What a non-trade report loses quietly](atoms/what-a-non-trade-report-loses-quietly.md) — the fields cleared from such a report and the fields worth complaining about are two lists that differ by one, so one field is changed with nothing said.
- [What the venue's own records are held to](atoms/what-the-venues-own-records-are-held-to.md) — a trade is checked for its timestamp alone while a record of money moving is checked three ways, and one record insists on exactly one of its two fields.
- [What a fill is](atoms/what-a-fill-is.md) — a report from a venue that some of an order has traded: an amount, at a price, at a time.
- [What every event is held to](atoms/what-every-event-is-held-to.md) — all forty-two kinds of recorded fact are built on one shared record, and each runs its two questions before its own: that the account be named, and that the time carry a zone that answers.
- [The records that reseal their own fields](atoms/the-records-that-reseal-their-own-fields.md) — a record that cannot be changed once made still rewrites its own lists as it is built, which protects the record from the caller's list but not from a list inside that list.
- [The floors a count is held to](atoms/the-floors-a-count-is-held-to.md) — two records carry the same two counts under the same names and disagree about the lowest value each may take, so whether zero is a fact one of them will carry depends on which one is carrying it.
- [The fields asked to be a true-or-false](atoms/the-fields-asked-to-be-a-true-or-false.md) — a yes-or-no value is demanded of two fields, turned away in four places because it also answers as a number, and left unexamined in two more.
- [The mode a scheme may not begin with](atoms/the-mode-a-scheme-may-not-begin-with.md) — the record of a scheme starting names one way of working it refuses and allows everything else, where every similar test names what it allows instead.
- [The names a record may leave out](atoms/the-names-a-record-may-leave-out.md) — a record can declare a name allowed to be missing, and the shared check then lets a missing one through while still turning away one written as an empty value.
- [The grounds a figure is weighed on](atoms/the-grounds-a-figure-is-weighed-on.md) — a number in a recorded fact may be asked what kind of thing it is, whether it is a real quantity, and which side of zero it falls on; most are asked only the last, which lets an unbounded value through.
- [The two noughts a figure is compared with](atoms/the-two-noughts-a-figure-is-compared-with.md) — the zero a number is compared against is written two different ways in one file, and which way it is decides whether a not-a-number value is quietly accepted or stops the record being built.
- [The records that weigh their own fields against each other](atoms/the-records-that-weigh-their-own-fields-against-each-other.md) — a handful of the records reject a combination their fields would each pass alone, among them a reported balance disagreement whose two figures agree, and an order asking to be sized in coin and in money at once.
- [The rule a bracket borrows](atoms/the-rule-a-bracket-borrows.md) — the record of a bracket starting builds a second record out of its own fields, keeps nothing of it, and gets its rule about the protective legs from the checks that second record runs.
- [What the shared checks pass over](atoms/what-the-shared-checks-pass-over.md) — the checks written once and used across the records are narrower than they look: an empty list of figures passes, and asking whether a value is one of the named ones is done three different ways that disagree.
- [Outcomes](atoms/what-an-outcome-is.md) — what Praxis reports back about a request: where it has got to, how much filled, and at what average.
- [Order types](atoms/order-types.md) — the kinds of order Praxis can send — at the market, at a named price, waiting on a trigger, and a linked pair where one filling cancels the other.
- [The prices an order must name](atoms/the-prices-an-order-must-name.md) — which kinds of order need a price, a trigger, or both.
- [Where the order type decides](atoms/where-the-order-type-decides.md) — the few places where the kind of order alone changes what Praxis does.
- [The order book](atoms/the-order-book.md) — what a venue publishes about a pair: buyers on one side, sellers on the other, each a price and an amount wanted.
- [Holdings](atoms/holdings.md) — what one trade holds: which way it went, how much, and the average price paid to build it.

## A decision becomes an order

- [How a decision becomes a command](atoms/how-a-decision-becomes-a-command.md) — a decision from Nexus is turned into an instruction Praxis will act on, field by field.
- [What a command must carry](atoms/what-a-command-must-carry.md) — one instruction must name the account, the trade and itself, and give either an amount of coin or an amount of money, never both.
- [The payload a mode's settings arrive in](atoms/the-payload-a-modes-settings-arrive-in.md) — the settings that ride along with a decision, and how they are turned into the ones the chosen way of working needs.
- [What a command's checks let through](atoms/what-a-commands-checks-let-through.md) — the checks on an instruction only ask whether something is there, or whether a number is above zero, so other mistakes pass and surface later.
- [What turns work away](atoms/what-turns-work-away.md) — the checks work must pass before Praxis records it at all, before it is ever queued.
- [The order a command's checks stand in](atoms/the-order-a-commands-checks-stand-in.md) — a request admitted into the trading side passes four checks in a fixed order, so a request wrong in two ways is told about whichever fault comes first rather than the worst one.
- [What a command sized in money is held to](atoms/what-a-command-sized-in-money-is-held-to.md) — saying how much to spend rather than how much to buy is allowed for one kind of order on one side, and the rules enforcing that sit in three separate places.
- [Which modes the money refusal reaches](atoms/which-modes-the-money-refusal-reaches.md) — the refusal that looks like a rule about every way of working cannot be met by the hidden order or the ladder, which earlier checks stop instead.
- [What a mode command refuses of itself](atoms/what-a-mode-command-refuses-of-itself.md) — a request naming any way of working but the simplest meets one rule that applies to all of them and three narrow ones, and some ways of working meet nothing but the first.
- [The venue rules that are never applied](atoms/the-venue-rules-that-are-never-applied.md) — half of what weighs a request at the door is written to take the venue's own trading rules, and the place that admits requests hands in none, so that half runs only under test.
- [What the per-child rules would weigh](atoms/what-the-per-child-rules-would-weigh.md) — the unapplied half checks a different number of things for each shape of split order, from one for an evenly spread order to four for a hidden one.
- [What the host refuses first](atoms/what-the-host-refuses-first.md) — one guard every way in shares, answered by a raise before anything is written down.

## Getting an order to the venue

- [Order placement](atoms/how-an-order-is-placed.md) — placing happens in two stages: recording the work and returning, then reaching the venue on a later pass.
- [How an order is named](atoms/how-an-order-is-named.md) — Praxis works an order's name out rather than drawing it at random, so the same inputs always give the same name.
- [The likely-price check](atoms/how-the-likely-price-is-checked.md) — refusing an order that would trade too far from the market price.
- [Estimating the likely price](atoms/how-the-likely-price-is-worked-out.md) — the average price Praxis expects an order to get, worked out by walking the offers standing at the venue.
- [The sizes a venue trades in](atoms/the-sizes-a-venue-trades-in.md) — a venue accepts only certain amounts and prices, and Praxis weighs an order against the figures it holds for that symbol — or sends it unweighed when it holds none.
- [What a venue must be able to do](atoms/what-a-venue-must-be-able-to-do.md) — the jobs every venue has to offer — register an account, place, cancel, report balances — written down as one list.
- [Holding back sends](atoms/holding-back-sends.md) — a limit on how fast Praxis may call the venue: most calls wait for a slot, and asking the time does not.
- [When Praxis retries a venue call](atoms/when-praxis-retries-a-venue-call.md) — a repeatable call gets three attempts and a send gets one, and each attempt spends a slot of the allowance.
- [When a send's outcome is unknown](atoms/when-a-send-is-unclear.md) — a call to the venue can fail without settling whether the order arrived, so Praxis asks the venue before deciding.
- [A reply that cannot be read](atoms/a-reply-that-cannot-be-read.md) — what happens when the venue answers in a shape Praxis cannot understand.
- [Who refused a send](atoms/who-refused-a-send.md) — a failed send is put down to the venue, to Praxis, or to neither, so a later reader need not judge the wording.
- [What a refusal says](atoms/what-a-refusal-says.md) — a refusal arrives as a named kind, and the kind decides whether the outcome is settled or must be asked after.

## The pass that takes work off the queues

- [The account worker](atoms/how-waiting-work-is-drained.md) — each account has one worker going round the same circuit until it is stopped, taking work off a queue.
- [The priority line](atoms/the-priority-line.md) — cancellations and changes wait in a line of their own, emptied on each circuit once the account has started, before anything waiting to be placed.
- [Work already under way](atoms/work-already-under-way.md) — what a pass moves along between taking one request and the next — a cancel tried again, a slice now due, protection waiting to go up.
- [When an account is reconciling or poisoned](atoms/when-an-account-is-reconciling-or-poisoned.md) — what a pass does for an account being brought back into step with the venue, or carrying a fault that needs a restart, and when there is no pass left to do it.
- [When a reconnection releases an account](atoms/when-a-reconnection-releases-an-account.md) — a gated account waits on its feed coming back, not on time passing.
- [A change waiting out a hold](atoms/a-change-waiting-out-a-hold.md) — a change to an order already sent is put aside while the account is catching up with the venue, and is never taken up at all once the worker has stopped.
- [How a worker stops taking work](atoms/how-a-worker-stops.md) — the loop an account takes its work from can die, be cancelled, or go on turning without ever reaching that work, and each leaves the account differently placed.

## Ways an order can be worked

- [Ways of working a host may drive](atoms/ways-of-working-a-host-may-drive.md) — a host is given the list of ways it may work an order, and work naming one outside that list is turned away.
- [How the mode is recomputed](atoms/how-the-mode-is-recomputed.md) — every change to what an account is allowed to do comes from one calculation over the holds placed on it and the latest health reading, and a hold can only make matters worse.
- [Which hold the mode is blamed on](atoms/which-hold-the-mode-is-blamed-on.md) — several reasons for a halt can stand at once and the record names one of them, chosen by rank rather than by which arrived first.
- [What setting a hold that already stands does](atoms/what-setting-a-hold-that-already-stands-does.md) — placing a hold that is already in place leaves its recorded reason and time untouched, so what a reason says is why the hold first went on.
- [What the two risk breakers do](atoms/what-the-two-risk-breakers-do.md) — the loss breaker lifts itself as losses decay and the drawdown breaker never lifts at all, so one halt clears by itself and the other waits for somebody.
- [The two reduce-only holds](atoms/the-two-reduce-only-holds.md) — besides the holds that stop an account outright, two ask only that it stop opening new positions, and both are outranked by every halting hold.
- [Why the health tick commits in one hold of the lock](atoms/why-the-health-tick-commits-in-one-hold-of-the-lock.md) — the regular check writes its reading and re-tests the loss limits without letting go in between, so no reader sees a half-settled answer that the same check then overwrites.
- [The halt notification that is dropped](atoms/the-halt-notification-that-is-dropped.md) — an alert about a halt is left as a note to be sent later, and a halt lifted before the note is read loses its alert rather than having it delayed.
- [What a restart re-derives](atoms/what-a-restart-re-derives.md) — a host coming back up throws away the hold its own shutdown placed, keeps the rest, and works out afresh what the account is allowed to do.
- [The settings each way of working takes](atoms/the-settings-each-way-of-working-takes.md) — what each way of working an order asks to be told, and the mistakes in it that get the order refused.
- [Work fed out over time](atoms/work-fed-out-over-time.md) — an amount broken into slices that go out one at a time with a gap between them.
- [Orders at several prices](atoms/orders-at-several-prices.md) — spreading an amount across named prices, an order each, sent one after another in one pass.
- [Part-shown orders](atoms/part-shown-orders.md) — one resting order the venue displays only part of at a time.
- [When a weight curve plans nothing](atoms/when-a-weight-curve-plans-nothing.md) — a curve that passes every settings check can still split an amount into a part of nothing, caught on the way out rather than at the start.
- [Protection](atoms/protection.md) — the pair of orders put up after an opening one, to close the position at a good price or at a bad one.
- [When protection is put up again](atoms/when-protection-is-put-up-again.md) — the protective pair goes up once and a second attempt is turned back — except where the prices are being changed, which sends a fresh pair past that guard.
- [How protection's prices are changed](atoms/how-protections-prices-are-changed.md) — the resting pair taken down and a fresh one put up for what is left, or nothing put up where nothing is worth protecting.
- [When protection is lost](atoms/when-protection-is-lost.md) — once the pair guarding a position counts as gone, the account stops adding to it, a closing order is attempted where the policy says so, and Nexus is told.
- [How an unfinished close is recovered](atoms/how-an-unfinished-close-is-recovered.md) — a start picks up a close that never settled from the failures written down, and weighs what is open again before sending.
- [When a flatten places nothing](atoms/when-a-flatten-places-nothing.md) — the order that closes an unprotected position is only an attempt: an unknown venue, a pair that may still be resting, or too little left to close stop it before anything goes out.
- [How big a flatten goes out](atoms/how-big-a-flatten-goes-out.md) — the remainder cut down to what the account can pay with and rounded to the venue's step, against a price worked out for that purpose alone.

## Changing, cancelling and giving up

- [Changing work already sent](atoms/changing-work-already-sent.md) — asking Praxis to alter work it has accepted, what may be altered depending on how that work is carried out.
- [The change each way of working accepts](atoms/the-change-each-way-of-working-accepts.md) — every request to alter work must name the command, the account and a reason, and a change to how work is spread over time is refused outright.
- [What each change insists on](atoms/what-each-change-insists-on.md) — which fields each way of working lets a change touch, and what it checks before touching them.
- [How a schedule change is carried out](atoms/how-a-schedule-change-is-carried-out.md) — the parts already sent kept, the rest planned again, and the next part set a full gap from now.
- [How a price change is carried out](atoms/how-a-price-change-is-carried-out.md) — a price change replaces the order rather than editing it: the resting one is cancelled, what it really filled is settled, and a fresh one goes out for the rest.
- [How a ladder's prices are changed](atoms/how-a-ladders-prices-are-changed.md) — the whole grid replaced: every old rung cancelled and seen to be over before one new rung goes out.
- [What stops a ladder's new rungs](atoms/what-stops-a-ladders-new-rungs.md) — a grid can be left short: nothing worth placing, a freeze, or a rung that halts the run each time it is tried.
- [When a price change leaves no new order](atoms/when-a-price-change-places-nothing.md) — a price change can end with nothing resting in place of the order it replaced: the cancel never settles, the order is still working, too little is left to trade, or the send goes out and comes back unconfirmed.
- [Cancellation](atoms/how-a-trade-is-cancelled.md) — asking Praxis to stop a request already submitted, answered at once or taken up in the line of its own.
- [Abandoning work outright](atoms/abandoning-work-outright.md) — asking Praxis to give up on one command rather than alter it, cancelling at the venue where an order is out.
- [Where an abandonment lands](atoms/where-an-abandonment-lands.md) — what giving up on a command does, by how far that command had got.

## What a fill does when it arrives

- [What a fill changes](atoms/what-a-fill-changes.md) — a fill updates the order's record and then the money's, so a failure on the first leaves the second alone.
- [Fills and the order](atoms/fills-and-the-order.md) — how a fill moves an order's totals and status, including one arriving too late.
- [How an event finds its way through the projection](atoms/how-an-event-finds-its-way-through-the-projection.md) — some kinds are handled and answered before the rest are tried in a single chain, and several kinds share one closing routine where a refused send and a refusal from the venue leave the order in the same final state, so the state alone does not say which happened.
- [What closing an order moves it to](atoms/what-closing-an-order-moves-it-to.md) — the order is moved out of the live book and into the closed one under the same name, so it stays readable afterwards and a reader looking in only one book can mistake a closed order for one that never existed.
- [The lookup every handler shares](atoms/the-lookup-every-handler-shares.md) — one routine finds an order for every handler, logs a warning when it finds none, and hands back what it found for the caller to judge; a single handler checks the closed book first and so stays quiet on a repeat.
- [What the projection copies under its lock](atoms/what-the-projection-copies-under-its-lock.md) — one lock guards the holdings alone and a reading of them returns a copy of each, while the orders and the schemes beside them are read with no lock at all.
- [When an update fails](atoms/when-an-update-fails.md) — failing to update the order's own totals stops the account; failing to update the money record is logged and passed over.
- [How an outside event enters the record](atoms/how-an-outside-event-enters-the-record.md) — news from outside is written by the account's own writer, and the caller waits to be told how it went.
- [Where duplicates stop](atoms/where-duplicates-stop.md) — the same fill can arrive from the send, from the live feed and from a later query — it is written down once, and only that copy is acted on.
- [The part of a fill a command counts](atoms/the-part-of-a-fill-a-command-counts.md) — the whole fill goes into the books, and only what fits the order's budget reaches the figures Nexus hears.

## What the venue tells Praxis

- [The venue feed](atoms/the-venue-feed.md) — a connection kept open per account, over which the venue says what happened to that account's orders unasked.
- [What opening the stream does and does not do](atoms/what-opening-the-stream-does-and-does-not-do.md) — asking for the connection twice is harmless and silent, and the part that reconnects after a break is only started where somebody is listening for messages.
- [What setting up tears down first](atoms/what-setting-up-tears-down-first.md) — setting the connection up lets go of whatever it already had before it checks anything, so a bad address costs the working connection as well as the complaint.
- [The address the stream will accept](atoms/the-address-the-stream-will-accept.md) — a secured address is always allowed and an unsecured one only where the setting naming the simulator holds something, and a setting holding only blank space counts as holding nothing.
- [How a subscription is signed and acknowledged](atoms/how-a-subscription-is-signed-and-acknowledged.md) — the request's values are put in a fixed order before being signed so one set of values gives one text, and the signature is added only afterwards.
- [The order closing the stream does things in](atoms/the-order-closing-the-stream-does-things-in.md) — the reconnecting is stopped, the connection is let go of before anything is sent, and only then is the venue told and the connection closed.
- [What a closing can raise](atoms/what-a-closing-can-raise.md) — each step of a close ignores the kind of problem it expects and passes anything else on, so some closes stop partway and leave the connection open.
- [The failures the reconnect loop swallows](atoms/the-failures-the-reconnect-loop-swallows.md) — the loop is built to survive the venue being unreachable for as long as it takes, and the interruption it cannot survive is exactly what stops it when the connection is closed on purpose.
- [The kept book](atoms/the-kept-book.md) — Praxis's own copy of the latest offers for a pair, held with the time it arrived so a check need not wait on the venue.
- [Backfilling missed trades](atoms/backfilling-missed-trades.md) — asking the venue for trades Praxis did not hear about, after the feed was away or at a start.
- [How an order is brought back into step](atoms/how-an-order-is-brought-back-into-step.md) — the venue asked what became of each working order, fills taken in before any ending is concluded.

## What Praxis reports back to Nexus

- [How an outcome crosses back](atoms/how-an-outcome-crosses-back.md) — Praxis reports running totals for a command and Nexus wants one event's figures, so one report becomes several or none.
- [The figures an outcome carries](atoms/the-figures-an-outcome-carries.md) — a report is refused unless its numbers hold together: a target above zero, a filled amount within it, and an average price that fits them.
- [The figures carried back on a fill](atoms/the-figures-carried-back-on-a-fill.md) — what Nexus is told on a fill is the change since the last report rather than the running total, and one change can cover several fills.
- [The figures an outcome reports](atoms/the-figures-an-outcome-reports.md) — the slice count is slices sent rather than slices finished, and how far along is the filled amount against the target.
- [What an outcome does not check](atoms/what-an-outcome-does-not-check.md) — a report is never checked for whether its value matches amount times price, nor for whether its state and its counts agree.
- [How an outcome is delivered](atoms/how-an-outcome-is-delivered.md) — an outcome is written down before it is handed over, and the handing over can fail.
- [What stops an outcome crossing twice](atoms/what-stops-an-outcome-crossing-twice.md) — once a request has ended, a later report of it is dropped — for as long as that ending is still remembered.
- [How an undelivered outcome is replayed](atoms/how-an-undelivered-outcome-is-replayed.md) — how the next startup works out which reports are not yet accounted for.
- [Who a restart tells again](atoms/who-a-restart-tells-again.md) — a report can have two receivers, and a restart resends to the first one only.
- [Outcomes that never come back](atoms/outcomes-that-never-come-back.md) — when a report being resent is given up on, and what is left behind when it is.

## When the books do not match the venue

- [The window a movement is found in](atoms/the-window-a-movement-is-found-in.md) — money moving in or out is looked for from the last one seen, and never from before the account was taken on.
- [The holds a report places](atoms/the-holds-a-report-places.md) — what a reported finding does to the account: halt it, block new entries, or log it, and only a hand from outside lifts the hold.

## What Nexus does with the money

- [The checks a decision passes](atoms/the-checks-a-decision-passes.md) — a given list walked in order until one refuses, and most of it skipped for an action that closes rather than commits.
- [What a check must answer with](atoms/what-a-check-must-answer-with.md) — two shapes an answer may take, and the answers the walk declines to interpret at all.
- [What becomes of money set aside](atoms/what-becomes-of-money-set-aside.md) — money held back for a trade that never happens stays held back until something else comes along and asks.
- [How far down the account is](atoms/how-far-down-the-account-is.md) — losses are measured both from the best the account has ever been worth and from the best its closed trades have ever added up to, and those are different numbers.
- [What the price check weighs](atoms/what-the-price-check-weighs.md) — the check that looks at the market stops a trade for a number being absent as readily as for one being out of bounds.
- [What the price records refuse](atoms/what-the-price-records-refuse.md) — the reading of the market and the record of limits each refuse what they are built from, and every field of the reading may be left out entirely.
- [The per-strategy caps, trimmed and sealed](atoms/the-per-strategy-caps-trimmed-and-sealed.md) — a limit set for one strategy is filed under its name with the padding removed, and two names that become the same once trimmed are refused rather than one quietly winning.
- [How the price limits are built from the configuration](atoms/how-the-price-limits-are-built-from-the-configuration.md) — one field is converted from seconds to milliseconds, the rest are passed through unexamined, and the record they are handed to is what weighs them.
- [Who is told about a price refusal](atoms/who-is-told-about-a-price-refusal.md) — a refusal is sorted into one of three groups that mark who should hear about it, and a reason nobody has sorted yet lands in the middle group by default.
- [What the platform ceilings weigh](atoms/what-the-platform-ceilings-weigh.md) — the last check before an order goes out asks for five numbers and is handed one, and the limits it offers on the rest cannot be used.
- [What each check weighs](atoms/what-each-check-weighs.md) — six checks stand between a strategy's decision and the venue, and they look at quite different things: the order itself, what the account has lost, and whether the venue is answering.
- [The roads around the checks](atoms/the-roads-around-the-checks.md) — some orders go out to the venue without passing a single one of the checks the rest pass.
- [What a strategy's action is checked for](atoms/what-a-strategys-action-is-checked-for.md) — a strategy's answer is one record whose every field is weighed on its own first, and leaving a field out is never what the record refuses at that stage.
- [What each kind of action requires](atoms/what-each-kind-of-action-requires.md) — once the fields are sound, the record checks what the kind of request needs, gathering every missing field into a single complaint rather than reporting them one attempt at a time.
- [When an entry may name a sum instead of a size](atoms/when-an-entry-may-name-a-sum-instead-of-a-size.md) — a request to open a position says how much either in coin or in money to spend, and the money form is allowed only for a plain purchase at whatever the market gives.
- [The two mappings an action reseals](atoms/the-two-mappings-an-action-reseals.md) — the record copies each set of settings it is handed and puts it behind a read-only view, so neither side can write into one afterwards, though a value nested inside one stays shared.
- [What an entry outcome changes](atoms/what-an-entry-outcome-changes.md) — a report of one kind moves the money set aside for a command on to the next place it sits, and two kinds of report move nothing at all.
- [What each outcome's answer records](atoms/what-each-outcomes-answer-records.md) — the reply to a report says whether it worked, and separately whether a holding or the money changed; a cancelled or refused sale can reply that it worked while recording that the money did not move.
- [What a position change checks](atoms/what-a-position-change-checks.md) — the routines that move a holding insist on different things: a missing number in the arriving report is checked one way and a missing holding another, and one check carries on regardless of what it finds.
- [What a settling fee does to the figure](atoms/what-a-settling-fee-does-to-the-figure.md) — the gap between a guessed trading cost and what the report says it came to moves two balances in opposite directions, and can raise the money held against a strategy without anyone committing more.
- [When a reservation times out](atoms/when-a-reservation-times-out.md) — money held for a trade that never went out is given back by a sweep at the start of a fresh request, and an order finding its own hold already expired hands the money back itself rather than waiting for that sweep.
- [Releasing an order whose record was lost](atoms/releasing-an-order-whose-record-was-lost.md) — where the system knows an order by name but has lost everything else about it, the money behind it is given back from the name alone, and asking about an order that was never there is treated as success.
- [Whether a fill closes a position](atoms/whether-a-fill-closes-a-position.md) — a completed trade is checked against the holding it was meant to close, and a leftover below the increment the venue prices sizes in counts as closed.
- [What weighs a shutdown sale](atoms/what-weighs-a-shutdown-sale.md) — an order the system approves itself still meets the guard comparing its price to the open market, and the check against the venue's own minimum size, though an order fed out in pieces misses the first of those entirely.
- [The sale a host sends as it stops](atoms/the-sale-a-host-sends-as-it-stops.md) — shutting down, the system asks each strategy what to sell and approves those orders itself, so the checks that normally stand in front of an order never see them.
- [Where an imported position's names come from](atoms/where-an-imported-positions-names-come-from.md) — a holding rebuilt at startup needs three labels off the side that placed the orders, and a holding missing any one of them is left out of the records altogether.
- [What a stopping host halts first](atoms/what-a-stopping-host-halts-first.md) — on the way down, anything a strategy asks for through the usual checks is turned away, and the background jobs are switched off one after another in a set order.
- [What a stopping host saves of its strategies](atoms/what-a-stopping-host-saves-of-its-strategies.md) — each strategy is asked to describe itself in bytes, which are written to a side file and then moved over the old one, so what is on disk is always one whole version.
- [What a failed save costs the stopping](atoms/what-a-failed-save-costs-the-stopping.md) — a write that fails on the disk loses one strategy's update while leaving what it saved before, a directory that cannot be made loses every update, and a strategy handing back something the writer cannot take costs the shutdown its final saved picture, though the account is still handed back.
- [What a start does with a strategy's saved state](atoms/what-a-start-does-with-a-strategys-saved-state.md) — the saved bytes are handed back to the strategy, with empty bytes standing in where the file is missing or unreadable, and a setting that is left blank means the start never looks.
- [The last record a stopping host writes](atoms/the-last-record-a-stopping-host-writes.md) — on the way out a picture of the state is saved and then the account is handed back, each wrapped so a failure is written down and passed over, and the handing back can fail with the account still registered.
- [What a checkpoint holds](atoms/what-a-checkpoint-holds.md) — the saved picture covers money, risk, holdings and trading state while what each strategy kept for itself goes to separate files; a stopping host, a timer and sometimes a start each ask for one.
- [What the thirty-day cut leaves](atoms/what-the-thirty-day-cut-leaves.md) — trimming the log goes by when each line was written, while the recent-loss figures rebuilt from what is left go by when the thing described happened, and the running total adds up what survived once repeats of one outcome are dropped.
- [What the first stage weighs before the mode](atoms/what-the-first-stage-weighs-before-the-mode.md) — a name with space around it, a request worth nothing, and a strategy whose whole allowance is nothing are each turned away before trading state is looked at, while a request to close or to alter an order is named as exempt from the amount test and the allowance test rather than having to clear them.
- [What settles a request before the first stage](atoms/what-settles-a-request-before-the-first-stage.md) — the name is fixed when the settings are read, while the money allowance is written as the order is put together — from two figures the settings already hold above zero, except on a request to alter an order, which is given no allowance at all.
- [What an entry's sum is worked out from](atoms/what-an-entrys-sum-is-worked-out-from.md) — an order to buy carries either an amount of money or a quantity, and a quantity is trimmed to what the venue trades in before it is priced, which can make the order smaller than was asked for.
- [What Praxis works out for an exit](atoms/what-praxis-works-out-for-an-exit.md) — before the checks see a request to close a holding, that holding is looked up, what is left of it after anything already in progress is computed, and a label is minted where none came.
- [The watch over handovers that timed out](atoms/the-watch-over-handovers-that-timed-out.md) — an order whose handover ran out of time is flagged as unsettled, and a job reports the flags that have stood too long; a final reply the books cannot take in drops the order off the list with its flag still raised, while one that blows up on the way in leaves both standing.
- [What the prediction loop's stop leaves running](atoms/what-the-prediction-loops-stop-leaves-running.md) — the job that reads predictions and hands them to the strategies gathers its work first and then carries it out, so work gathered before the switch-off is still carried out, and can still reach a strategy and put its answer up for the usual checks.
- [Which prediction row is used](atoms/which-prediction-row-is-used.md) — one row is taken out of a whole file of them: the unusable ones are dropped and the latest of what is left wins, whatever order the file held them in.
- [What the manifest must be](atoms/what-the-manifest-must-be.md) — every turn starts by reading the file that says where the predictions live, and a file that is merely the wrong shape is complained about while one that is broken stops the turn by failing.
- [How a close is found for a prediction](atoms/how-a-close-is-found-for-a-prediction.md) — a prediction says what to do and not at what price, so the price is looked up by an exact match on the moment, with no tolerance at all between the side that writes predictions and the side that writes prices.
- [What the cohort line records](atoms/what-the-cohort-line-records.md) — the log says which trained cohort served each turn, and reports a change of cohort only once it has something to compare against.
- [What a failure partway through a turn leaves behind](atoms/what-a-failure-partway-through-a-turn-leaves-behind.md) — the note of how far the loop got sits between handing a prediction to a strategy and sending what came back, so a failure before it has the work come round again and a failure after it does not.
- [How a value is made safe to log](atoms/how-a-value-is-made-safe-to-log.md) — a run of tests turns anything in a prediction into something a log line can carry, and what this loop actually sends it reaches only one of those tests.
- [Starting the loop, and one turn by hand](atoms/starting-the-loop-and-one-turn-by-hand.md) — nothing is served at the moment of starting, and the same turn asked for directly reports a failure to its caller where the scheduled one only logs it.
- [How a record reaches the log](atoms/how-a-record-reaches-the-log.md) — every line added to the log carries a short head saying how long it is and a number worked out from its contents, is forced out to the disk before the write is called done, and a file whose end was left torn is trimmed back to the last sound line before anything new goes on.
- [How a record is taken off the file](atoms/how-a-record-is-taken-off-the-file.md) — the file's length is noted before it is opened, the opening marker is read off the front, and each line is then found by reading a short fixed-size head that says how far the line runs; the scan that decides where to trim can leave its loop only by answering from inside it.
- [What packs a record and what forces a folder](atoms/what-packs-a-record-and-what-forces-a-folder.md) — a line going into the log is first turned into bytes as a set of named parts, among them its own position in the sequence, so a line read back says where it belongs; and the routine that makes a folder's own change stick keeps hold of nothing once it is done.
- [The version a saved record is read at](atoms/the-version-a-saved-record-is-read-at.md) — every saved record says which version of the writing made it, and one arriving without that marking is read as the oldest rather than turned away.
- [What happens to an event's version on the way in](atoms/what-happens-to-an-events-version-on-the-way-in.md) — an event's version marking is rounded down before anything looks at it, so a damaged marking is read by whichever reader the whole part names.
- [Which version an event is written at](atoms/which-version-an-event-is-written-at.md) — whether an event is written in the newer form turns on one field being filled in, and the newer reader insists on that field so firmly it can only ever be refusing something this code did not write.
- [What a malformed saved record is refused as](atoms/what-a-malformed-saved-record-is-refused-as.md) — anything that goes wrong while a saved record is being rebuilt comes back as one message, with the original failure's own words on the end and the failure itself still attached underneath.
- [The complaint folded into another](atoms/the-complaint-folded-into-another.md) — one reader writes a careful message about a bad field and the error handling around it swallows that message into a general one, and the case it was written for never reaches it.
- [The figures a risk record falls back to](atoms/the-figures-a-risk-record-falls-back-to.md) — a record of how far down an account is can be rebuilt from almost nothing, with one figure standing in for five others, though the writing always fills them all in.
- [What a snapshot from an earlier host is read as](atoms/what-a-snapshot-from-an-earlier-host-is-read-as.md) — a saved state gained fields over time, and reading an older one fills the gaps: some with nothing, some with a value that reads as all being well.
- [The small negative snapped to nought](atoms/the-small-negative-snapped-to-nought.md) — money arithmetic can leave a remainder a shade below zero, which would make a saved state unreadable, so most of the money figures are nudged back to zero as they are read.
- [The copies taken before a record is written](atoms/the-copies-taken-before-a-record-is-written.md) — the writing copies each list and mapping before walking it, which keeps each one whole but does not catch them all at a single moment.
- [What two writings of one state give](atoms/what-two-writings-of-one-state-give.md) — the same state written twice gives matching bytes for the sets of handled names and not for the mappings, which keep whatever order they were built in, so the bytes are no fingerprint of what was saved.
- [What a damaged record costs](atoms/what-a-damaged-record-costs.md) — reading the log stops at the first line that fails any of its checks, a short head, a length past the cap, a length past the file's end, a payload that runs out or a checksum that does not match and keeps only what came before it, so one bad line hides every good line written after it.
- [When a read raises instead of stopping](atoms/when-a-read-raises-instead-of-stopping.md) — a line whose contents cannot be turned back into a record makes the read fail outright rather than stop, so the caller is handed nothing at all instead of the good lines before it.
- [Which of a host's changes reach the log](atoms/which-of-a-hosts-changes-reach-the-log.md) — an operator halting or resuming the whole account is written down every time, and an outcome from the venue only where working it through succeeded and one of three marks is set, while the running valuations of what is held are written on nobody's account and ride along with whatever else is being saved.
- [How the checkpoint rewrites the log](atoms/how-the-checkpoint-rewrites-the-log.md) — trimming the log empties the file and writes the surviving lines back into that same file, so a failure part-way through leaves the file holding less than the history it stood for.
- [The timer that checkpoints while a host runs](atoms/the-timer-that-checkpoints-while-a-host-runs.md) — a background job saves a picture of the state at a fixed spacing, measuring each wait from the end of the last save, and a save that fails is passed over, the turn after it making a whole new one rather than finishing the last.
- [The cap on how fast entries may go in](atoms/the-cap-on-how-fast-entries-may-go-in.md) — a limit on how many entries this one check lets by in a second is shared by every strategy on one account, so a busy strategy can spend it all and leave the others turned away.
- [The window that turns a repeated entry away](atoms/the-window-that-turns-a-repeated-entry-away.md) — a strategy sending the same labelled order twice inside a second has the later one refused, and labels are remembered against the strategy that sent them, so two strategies using one label do not collide.
- [Which hosts the checkpoint timer runs on](atoms/which-hosts-the-checkpoint-timer-runs-on.md) — the job that writes a full picture of the state at a fixed spacing is switched on only for a live account; a run over bars that already happened builds the same machinery and never switches it on, so a full picture gets written on such a run only where its own start asks for one.
- [Which clock a record is stamped off](atoms/which-clock-a-record-is-stamped-off.md) — every line written to the log carries the real time of the machine, even on a run driven by a made-up clock, because the part that writes those lines is never handed that clock.
- [The road that empties the log](atoms/the-road-that-empties-the-log.md) — one way of clearing the file wipes it completely, opening mark and all, and nothing in either system ever asks for it; the next write would put the mark back rather than refuse the file.
- [What writes the name a success records](atoms/what-writes-the-name-a-success-records.md) — the label kept of a reply from the venue, so a repeat can be turned away, is never saved to disk on its own account; it goes down with whatever the next save of the state was asked for, so a host that stops with no save of any kind behind it leaves a log and a picture that never held the label, while the turning away of a repeat reads that label where it stands in memory, so the wait costs nothing until a restart.
- [What a failed checkpoint costs a start](atoms/what-a-failed-checkpoint-costs-a-start.md) — a start that cannot save the state gives up the whole start, and nothing between there and the account's own thread catches it; a run over past bars has no such thread, and shuts the account down before the failure leaves.
- [How a command's unwritten mark is let go](atoms/how-a-commands-unwritten-mark-is-let-go.md) — a note that an instruction's figures have moved in memory without being saved yet is cleared in batches, so one instruction's save clears the notes standing against others, while a save an operator asks for clears none of them.
- [What can stop a closing record going down](atoms/what-can-stop-a-closing-record-going-down.md) — the line written to say a reply was taken, or that Praxis gave up on it, is handed to a background worker and waited on for ten seconds; the wait running out leaves the write still going, and on one road the line is never attempted at all.
- [What stops a start squaring its holdings](atoms/what-stops-a-start-squaring-its-holdings.md) — a start weighs its own record of what is held against what the trading side reports, and a holding the trading side does not mention ends the start rather than being adjusted away.
- [Where the figures a start squares start from](atoms/where-the-figures-a-start-squares-start-from.md) — each running total a start compares begins empty: one is carried on the record it recovers, and the other is built up as it reads what the trading side reports.
- [The road an arriving report takes](atoms/the-road-an-arriving-report-takes.md) — a reply from the venue is converted, has its figures applied in memory and is put on the account's queue, reading nothing of what an earlier run settled; an account with no queue has the reply dropped, and one whose parts are not yet assembled has the figures applied later instead.
- [When the store writes without its locks](atoms/when-the-store-writes-without-its-locks.md) — the part that saves state is handed the locks covering what it reads only after the start has already saved once, so that early save runs with none of them held.
- [Which of the store's writes take which locks](atoms/which-of-the-stores-writes-take-which-locks.md) — the saves it makes differ in what they lock: a save of the whole state holds the locks over what it reads, while a save of a single event holds none itself and leans on its caller to hold part of them.
- [What the walk that lets marks go cannot reach](atoms/what-the-walk-that-lets-marks-go-cannot-reach.md) — the notes saying an instruction's figures are unsaved are cleared from a list copied before the save begins, so a note added while that save runs is not looked at by it; that clearing is the only thing anywhere that removes a note.
- [How a value is written out and read back](atoms/how-a-value-is-written-out-and-read-back.md) — exact decimal figures go to storage as text and are rebuilt from that text, so no precision is lost, while anything the writer cannot handle throws rather than being stored in some other shape.
- [What opening the spine does to its schema](atoms/what-opening-the-spine-does-to-its-schema.md) — a file written by a later build is refused rather than read, an up-to-date one has its version left untouched, and the whole bringing-forward is committed once with any failure rolled back.
- [What the spine asks of its own identity](atoms/what-the-spine-asks-of-its-own-identity.md) — it refuses a file whose recorded chain settings differ from the build's, saying something different when a setting is missing than when it disagrees, because a lost record and a foreign chain are different problems.
- [The three shapes a coercion looks inside](atoms/the-three-shapes-a-coercion-looks-inside.md) — a field that allows several types always becomes the first of them, a list or pair turns every item into the first type it names, and a nested record keeps only the fields it still declares.
- [How an event is rebuilt from what was stored](atoms/how-an-event-is-rebuilt-from-what-was-stored.md) — a stored event whose type the code no longer has stops the read, while a stored field the type no longer declares is dropped without a word, so stale records fail loudly and stale fields vanish quietly.
- [What the money's lock is doing when a result is written](atoms/what-the-moneys-lock-is-doing-when-a-result-is-written.md) — the line recording what a sale came to is written with that lock held by no one, reached two ways: where the sale frees money set aside, the lock is handed back as the freeing finishes; where it frees none, nothing reaches for the lock at all.
- [What a write of the whole state waits on](atoms/what-a-write-of-the-whole-state-waits-on.md) — once the locks covering the account have been handed over, saving the whole picture takes whichever of them the money works under, so it waits on whichever of the money's own jobs is holding it; the job that sets money aside holds it from the moment it takes it until it answers.
- [The wrapper every strategy sits behind](atoms/the-wrapper-every-strategy-sits-behind.md) — each strategy is wrapped on its own, and that wrapper holds a lock which makes the strategy answer one thing at a time, while two different strategies can be answering at the same moment.
- [Which roads reach a strategy](atoms/which-roads-reach-a-strategy.md) — every occasion on which a strategy is spoken to, each arriving outside the tests from a single place in the code, and none of them named by Praxis's own code.
- [What a strategy must provide](atoms/what-a-strategy-must-provide.md) — what a strategy has to be able to do before it can be built at all, and the behaviour that is filled in for it — replaying what the log kept — whose filling throws away the record it is given.
- [What the router refuses](atoms/what-the-router-refuses.md) — the names turned away when something asks for a strategy by name, and the mistakes that stop the whole set of strategies being assembled in the first place.
- [What a turn of a timer does](atoms/what-a-turn-of-a-timer-does.md) — a timer fires once, and a turn of it checks whether the loop is still running before fetching what the strategy needs and again afterwards; a check that finds it stopped takes the turn no further, and the request for a successor builds nothing once the loop has stopped.
- [What a strategy is handed](atoms/what-a-strategy-is-handed.md) — the thing a strategy is given on the roads that hand it one is refused if the holdings are not a fixed run of them, if anything in that run is not a holding, if the money figure is not a finite decimal at or above nought, or if the mode is not a named one; once built, nothing on it can be swapped for something else.
- [What a command is checked for](atoms/what-a-command-is-checked-for.md) — the instruction Nexus hands the trading side checks itself as it is made: what must be present and usable, what may be left out but is rejected when present and wrong, and the deadline given as a true-or-false, which a test placed ahead of the whole-number test catches.
- [The marker a command's moment must carry](atoms/the-marker-a-commands-moment-must-carry.md) — the timestamp on that instruction is rejected unless it was built with the standard universal-time marker, so a zone that sits at the same offset is turned away, while the other handoffs the same connector makes accept it.
- [The settings a command rewrites](atoms/the-settings-a-command-rewrites.md) — where mode-specific settings are supplied, the instruction takes a locked copy and keeps that instead of what it was given, and nothing can afterwards be added to that copy, taken out of it, or put in place of what a key already holds.
- [What each kind of command must carry](atoms/what-each-kind-of-command-must-carry.md) — a new order must name a side, a self-trade setting and either a size or a spend but not both, while a change and a cancel must carry none of those nor any of the execution details, each kind checked against its own copy of the list.
- [What an outcome is checked for](atoms/what-an-outcome-is-checked-for.md) — the report the trading side sends back is rejected unless both its references are usable text, its type is a recognised one and its timestamp carries the standard universal-time marker, with the amount still working checked for every type after the report has already been sorted by whether anything filled.
- [What a fill must carry](atoms/what-a-fill-must-carry.md) — a report saying something filled must give the quantity, the price, the money value and the fee charged, each a finite figure and the first three above zero where the fee may be zero, and a report of any other kind must give none of the four.
- [The reason an outcome may carry](atoms/the-reason-an-outcome-may-carry.md) — a rejection has to explain itself in words that are not just blank space, a report saying the order was called off may explain itself or stay silent, and either explanation on a report of any other type is turned away.
- [The two measures an outcome reports](atoms/the-two-measures-an-outcome-reports.md) — two further numbers may each be supplied or omitted, an omitted one is passed over before the number is examined, and a supplied one is turned away unless the report says something filled, where it must be a finite number.
- [What money held for an order is checked for](atoms/what-money-held-for-an-order-is-checked-for.md) — the record of capital set aside is rejected unless its own identifier and the requesting strategy's are both usable text, and unless each of its two amounts is a real number, with each amount check rejecting a figure below zero rather than one standing at it.
- [The two moments a hold carries](atoms/the-two-moments-a-hold-carries.md) — that record stores when it was created and when it lapses, each needing the standard universal-time marker, and the lapse must fall strictly later, so a record cannot lapse at the instant it was created.
- [What a hold is asked after it exists](atoms/what-a-hold-is-asked-after-it-exists.md) — once the record is built, two things are read off it: its amounts added together, worked out afresh each time and named by none of its own checks, and whether it has lapsed, which rejects a supplied time lacking the standard marker and answers yes exactly at the lapse.
- [What the record of an attempt must carry](atoms/what-the-record-of-an-attempt-must-carry.md) — the result of a request for capital must pair a yes with a filled record field and an empty explanation, or a no with a filled explanation and an empty record field, as four separate checks, none of which examines the type of any of its three fields.
- [What a note for an outcome is checked for](atoms/what-a-note-for-an-outcome-is-checked-for.md) — the note carried alongside an order is rejected unless its two required identifiers are usable text, its direction is a recognised one, its two true-or-false fields really are, and its money amount and expected fee are real numbers with the amount above zero.
- [The size a note may leave out](atoms/the-size-a-note-may-leave-out.md) — the quantity may be omitted only when the order adds to a holding, an omission clearing that one check; a quantity that is supplied must be a real number above zero, and those two tests ignore the field that allowed the omission.
- [The flag saying an order grows a position](atoms/the-flag-saying-an-order-grows-a-position.md) — that metadata states whether the order adds to a holding, nothing compares the statement with the buy-or-sell direction, the opposite reading is worked out on demand, and a record setting both that statement and the other true-or-false field it carries is rejected by that check, which either of them standing false clears.

- [What a strategy's settings ask and answer](atoms/what-a-strategys-settings-ask-and-answer.md) — user-supplied configuration is rejected unless it is a dictionary, nothing inside it is examined, and asking for a setting it lacks returns whatever default the caller named.
- [What a lost protection is reported with](atoms/what-a-lost-protection-is-reported-with.md) — the notice that a bracket's safety orders could not be restored walks its account, its own identifier, the entry's identifier and the reason by name requiring usable text in each, requires a timestamp with the standard universal-time marker, and requires a whole number of one or more for the attempt count, with a true-or-false caught by a test placed ahead of the whole-number test.
- [The limits that may be left out](atoms/the-limits-that-may-be-left-out.md) — three configured risk limits may each be omitted and an omitted one is skipped before its own value is examined, while a limit that is supplied must be a finite decimal above zero, so zero is rejected and omission is the only way to leave one unset.
- [What a fill's own record requires](atoms/what-a-fills-own-record-requires.md) — a loop holds most of its identifiers to a shared non-empty check, its timestamp must carry a zone that reports an offset, and the venue's own trade identifier is absent from that loop, so it may be empty — which switches the duplicate-detection key from that identifier alone to four other fields combined.
- [What a fill's own record never asks](atoms/what-a-fills-own-record-never-asks.md) — its quantity and price are rejected at or below zero and its fee below zero, with no check requiring either figure or the fee to be a real number rather than an infinity, and the direction and the maker flag are never examined.

- [The records that refuse nothing](atoms/the-records-that-refuse-nothing.md) — three records declare their fields and validate none of them, so no rule constrains any value they hold; one of them can say whether its lifecycle has finished, by testing its state against the finished ones.
- [The order the health stage's checks stand in](atoms/the-order-the-health-stages-checks-stand-in.md) — a figure offered to the checks on the trading side's condition is turned away for what kind of thing it is before its size is ever looked at, and a whole field is turned away for its shape before anything inside it is read.
- [The orderings the health stage's policy insists on](atoms/the-orderings-the-health-stages-policy-insists-on.md) — each measurement's alert, act and halt limits have to run one way, and which way depends on the measurement; leaving the middle limit out means the outer two are never put side by side.

- [What the health policy refuses](atoms/what-the-health-policy-refuses.md) — the configured limits are each rejected unless they are real numbers of zero or more, each measurement's limits must not decrease across alert, act and halt, equal limits are accepted, and the proportions are capped at one by a test that runs after the ordering ones.
- [How the mode is settled](atoms/how-the-mode-is-settled.md) — the measurements are compared with their halting limits first and then with their acting limits, a measurement exactly at a limit counts as past it, and the answer is whichever measurement is doing worst rather than any combination of them.
- [The thresholds nothing weighs against](atoms/the-thresholds-nothing-weighs-against.md) — the alerting limit of each measurement is validated when the configuration is built and read by the ordering test, and then compared with no measurement anywhere, so changing one alters what configuration is accepted and nothing about what gets decided.
- [What stops a health turn](atoms/what-stops-a-health-turn.md) — the interval between ticks is rejected at zero or below but let through for infinity or a not-a-number, because the test compares instead of asking for a real number; a failed loss refresh is logged and the tick continues, while a failed fetch or a failed evaluation abandons it and leaves the mode alone.
- [The two roads a turn's answer takes](atoms/the-two-roads-a-turns-answer-takes.md) — without a controller the tick writes its answer straight through, doing nothing when the answer matches and otherwise replacing the whole record; with one, the answer is stored and the mode recomputed over every standing hold, so the result can differ from what the tick asked for.
- [What the records of the mode refuse](atoms/what-the-records-of-the-mode-refuse.md) — a single hold needs a true-or-false flag, text for its reason and a timestamp or nothing, and may claim to be holding without saying since when; the mode record needs a recognised mode and a usable driver name; and the records that gather holds validate nothing at all.
- [The moments with no marker](atoms/the-moments-with-no-marker.md) — the timestamp on the mode record and a hold’s own timestamp are each checked only for being timestamps, with no zone required and the mode record defaulting to the earliest possible instant, where the records crossing to the other side are rejected without the standard universal-time marker.
- [What the translation refuses](atoms/what-the-translation-refuses.md) — turning a passed decision into an instruction is refused for a decision that did not pass, a timestamp without the standard universal-time marker, an empty command identifier, or a modify action named outright, with a lookup table standing as the last barrier past those.
- [The kind of command nothing builds](atoms/the-kind-of-command-nothing-builds.md) — three command kinds are declared and the translation reaches two, so the amend kind is built nowhere on this side, though the command record still carries the rules for one.
- [What an exit is given where it asked for nothing](atoms/what-an-exit-is-given-where-it-asked-for-nothing.md) — an exit missing its execution style, order kind or deadline is handed a single shot, a market order and sixty seconds, where an entry is handed nothing and a non-order action has all three set aside; a spend and a quantity can never both be present.
- [What a funds movement refuses](atoms/what-a-funds-movement-refuses.md) — the record of money entering or leaving an account is rejected unless its two identifiers are usable text, its timestamp carries the standard universal-time marker, its amount is a finite decimal above zero, and its direction matches one of two words exactly, case included.
- [What a mismatch refuses](atoms/what-a-mismatch-refuses.md) — the record of a disagreement with the venue is rejected unless its three identifiers are usable text, its timestamp carries the standard universal-time marker, and both figures are finite decimals — and rejected when those figures agree, so it cannot be made to report that nothing is wrong.
- [The named values copied from the other side](atoms/the-named-values-copied-from-the-other-side.md) — the execution styles, order kinds and maker preferences are declared again on this side so the strategy layer need not import the trading package, which means each list agrees with its counterpart only while both are maintained, and nothing checks that they do.
- [What an instance's configuration refuses](atoms/what-an-instances-configuration-refuses.md) — the account and venue must be usable text, the duplicate window a whole number above zero with a true-or-false rejected, the optional rate and staleness limits held to that same rule, the optional spread and deviation limits finite decimals of zero or more, the self-trade setting a recognised one, and the shutdown timeouts finite numbers above zero.
- [The mapping this record accepts and its neighbours do not](atoms/the-mapping-this-record-accepts-and-its-neighbours-do-not.md) — the configuration takes any object that behaves as a mapping, where the signal and the strategy settings insist on a dictionary and reject anything else, so which objects a caller may pass depends on which record receives them.
- [The keys this record rewrites](atoms/the-keys-this-record-rewrites.md) — the configuration's per-strategy caps and its capital shares each trim their keys as they read them, reject one left with nothing, and reject one that collides with a key already taken after trimming, so a mapping that would have silently lost an entry is turned back instead.
- [The one check that reads a mapping whole](atoms/the-one-check-that-reads-a-mapping-whole.md) — each capital share must be a finite decimal above zero and no more than a hundred, and the shares are summed as they are read with a total above a hundred rejected, which is the only place this record examines entries as a group rather than one at a time.
- [The field that must name its yardstick](atoms/the-field-that-must-name-its-yardstick.md) — the reference price source is optional, trimmed and lower-cased and written back over itself, and accepted only as one specific name; a deviation limit of either kind is rejected when no source is named, which is the only place this record compares one of its fields with another.
- [What the loader asks of a path](atoms/what-the-loader-asks-of-a-path.md) — a strategy file must be named by a relative path ending in the module suffix, and the path is resolved before being tested for staying inside the base directory, so both a path climbing out and a link pointing out are caught; past that, the file must exist.
- [What the loader asks of what it found](atoms/what-the-loader-asks-of-what-it-found.md) — the module is run with any failure wrapped as one error naming the file, then it must export something under the expected name that is a class inheriting from the base and is not the base itself, and building the class wraps any failure as one error naming the strategy instead.
- [What a record of processing refuses](atoms/what-a-record-of-processing-refuses.md) — the record of how one report was handled needs a recognised kind, an explanation when it reports failure and none when it reports success, while the field those two checks read is never required to be a true-or-false, so text put there is read for truth alone.
- [What an entry in the log refuses](atoms/what-an-entry-in-the-log-refuses.md) — a single log record needs a recognised kind, a timestamp with no zone required, and bytes for its payload, while its ordering number is rejected only below zero and never checked for type, so a true-or-false or a fraction stands as a number there.
- [The two cuts across the kinds of outcome](atoms/the-two-cuts-across-the-kinds-of-outcome.md) — six outcome kinds are answered by two questions that do not line up: four count as finished and two as having filled, with the full fill answering yes to both and the acknowledgement yes to neither.
- [What stands where nothing was set](atoms/what-stands-where-nothing-was-set.md) — a signal hands a missing key back to the caller's own fallback, while the risk limits are simply empty until set so an unconfigured policy disables every breaker, and neither record notes anywhere that a default was taken.
- [The questions a record answers about itself](atoms/the-questions-a-record-answers-about-itself.md) — four records answer something about themselves in a single line: which accounts increase on the debit side and which do not, whether a command or an order was sized in money (answered by the presence of a money amount rather than a flag), and whether a position has closed (answered by its quantity equalling zero).
- [The escape in the shared check for a name](atoms/the-escape-in-the-shared-check-for-a-name.md) — one check holds every identifier on this side: it lets a missing value through when the caller marked the field optional, otherwise rejects a missing value and an empty string together, and never trims — so an identifier of spaces alone is kept here and rejected on the other side.
- [What a clock refuses and what stands in](atoms/what-a-clock-refuses-and-what-stands-in.md) — the replay clock rejects a start with no zone and keeps whatever zone it is given without converting it, while a price store given no clock uses one reporting the current moment in universal time.
- [The severity an alert gives back](atoms/the-severity-an-alert-gives-back.md) — telling an alert returns the severity actually recorded after an unknown one is turned into a warning, and the call that also posts reads that return rather than its own argument, so the logged line and the posted payload agree.
- [What the health report refuses](atoms/what-the-health-report-refuses.md) — the report of how the venue is answering rejects any of four measurements that is not a real number of zero or more, caps each proportion it carries at one, and holds the consecutive-failure tally to a separate whole-number rule, with a true-or-false rejected wherever a number is wanted.
- [The same record written twice](atoms/the-same-record-written-twice.md) — that report exists as separate code on each side of the boundary, holding the same measurements to the same rules; running both gives the same answers, neither names the other, and nothing compares them, so a rule changed on one side alone would show only when a report accepted on one side was rejected on the other.
- [What the bucket of permissions refuses](atoms/what-the-bucket-of-permissions-refuses.md) — the pacer for venue calls rejects a rate or a capacity at or below zero, starts out holding its capacity so early calls go straight through, and on every take adds the elapsed time times the rate while capping the total at the capacity, so a long idle spell buys no unlimited burst.
- [The token taken before the waiting](atoms/the-token-taken-before-the-waiting.md) — a take computes its wait, subtracts its token whether one was available or not, then releases the lock and waits, so the count can go negative and lengthen later waits; a wait cut short part way adds the token back so nobody pays for a call never made.
- [What a ladder's prices must be](atoms/what-a-ladders-prices-must-be.md) — each price must be a positive real decimal, and the prices must rise throughout or fall throughout, which allows a descending ladder and rejects a repeated price without any check looking for duplicates.
- [What a ladder's weights must come to](atoms/what-a-ladders-weights-must-come-to.md) — weights are optional and the checking stops where they are absent; supplied, they must match the prices in count, each be a positive real decimal, and together come to exactly one, which is the only test that reads them as a set.
- [The quantity a lot step leaves behind](atoms/the-quantity-a-lot-step-leaves-behind.md) — an even split reaches the total exactly when no trading step is known, because the final part takes the remainder whole; where a step is known the final part is rounded down like the rest, so the parts can come to less than was asked for, bounded by one step since a final part rounding to zero is rejected.
- [What a book poller refuses and answers](atoms/what-a-book-poller-refuses-and-answers.md) — the poller keeping the kept prices fresh rejects an empty symbol and an interval at or below zero by comparison rather than by type, reports itself running while its task exists and has not finished, refuses a second start by reading that report, and clears its stop signal before making the task so a stopped poller can start again.
- [The gap between polls](atoms/the-gap-between-polls.md) — the pause between polls is a bounded wait on the stop signal rather than a sleep, so the interval is the longest the pause can be and a stop is noticed immediately instead of after it, which is how this loop differs in shape from the others.
- [The stop that waits for its pollers](atoms/the-stop-that-waits-for-its-pollers.md) — stopping one poller waits for the turn in hand and swallows the interruption it caused, and the host walks all of them waiting for each under a single ten-second limit shared between them, keeping the list when the walk fails or runs out and emptying it only when the walk finishes.
- [What counts as one trade](atoms/what-counts-as-one-trade.md) — a replay's per-trade figures rest on bars grouped by a running count of the entry marks, with one trade being the held bars sharing a number and its return reached by multiplying its bars' returns rather than adding them, so two bars of a tenth give just over a fifth.
- [How the bars are cut into days](atoms/how-the-bars-are-cut-into-days.md) — the same bars are also filed by calendar day and reported oldest first, each day carrying its compounded return in basis points and that return divided by the share of its bars holding a position, which is given as nothing at all for a day that held none.
- [How a fall from a peak is recorded](atoms/how-a-fall-from-a-peak-is-recorded.md) — a running peak that only rises measures each bar's fall, and one test with three arms opens an episode on the first fall, deepens it while the fall worsens, and closes it on recovery with a depth in basis points and a length in days.
- [The falls that never ended](atoms/the-falls-that-never-ended.md) — a fall still open when the walk ends records its depth and no length, so the list of depths and the list of lengths need not come to the same size and cannot be paired off position by position past that point.
- [What recording a request refuses](atoms/what-recording-a-request-refuses.md) — the tracker rejects a latency that is not a real number of zero or more and a success flag that is not strictly a true-or-false, so an infinity, a not-a-number and a whole number of one are all turned back, which is stricter than the records around it.
- [What the lock covers](atoms/what-the-lock-covers.md) — one lock guards the latency store, the outcome store and the failure run; recording takes it to append to both stores and to move the run, while producing a report takes it only to copy them, releasing it before the slow-end latency and the failed share are worked out from those copies.
- [The moments a replay request names](atoms/the-moments-a-replay-request-names.md) — a window edge given with a trailing capital Z is rewritten to an explicit zero offset and anything else is passed to the standard reader untouched, so a lower-case z is refused, a bare date becomes midnight with no zone, and whether a missing zone is caught by the ordering comparison or later by the frame reader depends on what the other edge carries.
- [What stopping the replay listener does](atoms/what-stopping-the-replay-listener-does.md) — the shutdown attached to the server's cleanup waits for the run already under way and cancels whatever is still queued at that moment, and a cancelled run's record is never written again, so it still answers as running for as long as the service can be asked.
- [What the series are read as](atoms/what-the-series-are-read-as.md) — the predictions and the price columns are converted to floating-point numbers before anything examines them, so a boolean or a numeric string satisfies the check that a prediction be zero or one, while a value with no numeric form aborts the whole call as a prediction and, as a price, makes that bar untradable and splits any position spanning it into two separately costed trades.
- [Where the peak begins](atoms/where-the-peak-begins.md) — the high-water mark a drawdown is measured against is seeded at the stake the equity curve starts from rather than at the curve's own opening value, so a curve already below that stake at its first evaluated bar is recorded as being in a drawdown right there.
- [The day a bar is filed under](atoms/the-day-a-bar-is-filed-under.md) — the grouping uses the UTC calendar date of each bar's own opening timestamp, which the sole caller supplies, whereas the window those bars were selected by was filtered on their closing timestamps, so a bar opening before midnight and closing after it is grouped into the earlier date.
- [The window a backfill opens](atoms/the-window-a-backfill-opens.md) — with no stored cursor a page is requested by whatever start timestamp was supplied and no end timestamp is ever sent, so the request is open-ended at the far side; only a full page of parseable ids sets a cursor, and once one is set every later page is requested by cursor instead.
- [What an even split refuses](atoms/what-an-even-split-refuses.md) — the quantity is checked for being an exact finite positive Decimal and the child count only for being at least two, so a fractional count slips past and is rejected by the arithmetic instead, and where the venue's lot size is unknown the children are the quantity over the count with no minimum child size at all, the last taking the remainder of what the division produced rather than restoring digits the division dropped.
- [What a start reads back from a checkpoint](atoms/what-a-start-reads-back-from-a-checkpoint.md) — the stored state is read first and then overlaid by whatever state-change records the log still holds, a missing snapshot file reading as nothing at all rather than as an error, while the per-strategy loss figures are recomputed from the surviving events and the peak figures kept at whichever is larger, stored or recomputed.
- [What a weighted split asks of its weights](atoms/what-a-weighted-split-refuses.md) — the quantity and the number of weights are checked exactly as in the even split while the weight values are used untested, their type, sign and sum being enforced instead by whichever parameter record supplies them, and the final weight is never read at all because the final child is simply the quantity's remainder.
- [What a risk limit must be](atoms/what-a-risk-limit-must-be.md) — each configured limit may be omitted, and one that is supplied has to be a finite Decimal of zero or more; omitting a limit skips only its comparison, since every metric is computed and paired with its limit beforehand, and the comparison is strict, so a metric sitting exactly on its limit is allowed.
- [The levels the risk stage never reaches](atoms/the-levels-the-risk-stage-never-reaches.md) — the walk over the account's losses returns either a breach or nothing, never a warning or a halt, so the branch that converts a breach or a halt into a rejection has one arm that cannot fire here, though the identically written branch in the health stage has both live.
- [What counts as raising what a buy commits](atoms/what-counts-as-raising-what-a-buy-commits.md) — a sell amendment is cleared immediately, a ladder is routed elsewhere, and everything else is judged on the commitment prices listed for its execution mode, which only a single order and an iceberg have; a named price above the original, or one with no original to bound it, is rejected, while a stop-limit price and a row-of-rungs comparison sit in the deciding as paths nothing can reach.
- [How a ladder's change is weighed](atoms/how-a-ladders-change-is-weighed.md) — a buy's ladder amendment is compared as one quantity-weighted price total against another, each side taking amended values where amended and original ones otherwise, a side with no weights giving every rung an equal share, and shares that do not match the new price count being rejected outright.
- [What the capital stage refuses](atoms/what-the-capital-stage-refuses.md) — a reservation lifetime supplied to the stage has to be an integer above zero, with a boolean caught by a test of its own because the integer test would accept it, an omitted lifetime passing nothing on so the controller's default stands, and a denial whose reason is empty or blank being given a standing message.
- [The name a strategy answers to](atoms/the-name-a-strategy-answers-to.md) — the base keeps the identifier stripped of surrounding whitespace and rejects a whitespace-only one, unlike the shared helper Praxis names its records by, and the router strips when filing and when looking up, so a call naming a strategy with padding still finds it.
- [Starting and stopping the timers](atoms/starting-and-stopping-the-timers.md) — starting is ignored while the loop already runs and otherwise schedules one countdown per declared timer from a mapping copied at construction, stopping clears the flag and cancels every pending countdown, and a countdown already firing is never cancelled: it reads the flag itself, before it calls the strategy.
- [What the walk is built from](atoms/what-the-walk-is-built-from.md) — the stage order must be a tuple holding every stage exactly once, the checks must be a mapping with a callable entry for each stage the order names, and entries the order does not name are never examined, so one that is uncallable is neither rejected nor retained.
- [What the mark sampler is built with](atoms/what-the-mark-sampler-is-built-with.md) — the account and symbol are tested for truthiness only, so a whitespace name is kept, and the interval is rejected at or below zero without any check of its type, so a not-a-number interval makes the inter-sample wait expire immediately and a Decimal one makes that wait raise, ending the loop's task.

- [The interval a checkpoint timer takes](atoms/the-interval-a-checkpoint-timer-takes.md) — the interval is rejected unless it is an int or float above zero, with a boolean caught by a test of its own, while finiteness is never required, so a not-a-number interval is stored; starting is ignored while the timer runs, and the running flag is read again both at each tick and at each rescheduling.
- [What the translator remembers](atoms/what-the-translator-remembers.md) — the ceiling on remembered finished commands must be a positive integer and a boolean true passes it, bounding the list at one; finishing a command files its id, evicts the oldest ids past the ceiling and discards its own record, and an outcome for an id already evicted is treated as a brand-new command.
- [The words an ending carries](atoms/the-words-an-ending-carries.md) — a rejection supplies a stock word where Praxis recorded nothing or an empty string, but a whitespace-only reason is truthy here and is passed through to a record that requires something after stripping, so building it raises; an expiry crosses with no reason and any other ending carries whatever Praxis had.
- [The command that ends twice](atoms/the-command-that-ends-twice.md) — when the venue reports a bracket's protective order cancelled, that bracket's exit command is ended and dropped, and the flatten following an unprotected remainder derives the same command id, reinstalls it, and reports its fill with no check that the id already ended.
- [How a replay settles a bar](atoms/how-a-replay-settles-a-bar.md) — each bar ends by waiting for the account's command queue to empty and then draining outcomes until a round drains none, with eight rounds allowed before it raises, and the wait covers only the queue, so a scheme whose next slice is due later has already left it and arrives during a later bar.
- [The mode a replay gives itself](atoms/the-mode-a-replay-gives-itself.md) — a replay writes the ACTIVE mode into the account state itself, under the positions lock and before the first bar, because no health loop runs to move it; the trigger is recorded as the replay and the moment comes from the replay's own clock.
- [Registering an account, and undoing it](atoms/registering-an-account-and-undoing-it.md) — an empty account id and an account with no configured credentials are both refused, an already-registered account only has its venue credentials reapplied, and a new one is registered at the venue before the trading side, with the venue registration rolled back for two named failure kinds and left standing for any other.
- [What a simulated walk refuses](atoms/what-a-simulated-walk-hands-back.md) — both walks over binsim's book reject a non-finite or non-positive figure and answer an empty side with a different error naming the book as never filled, and the spend walk's second return value reports the unspent budget only when the book ran out, being forced to zero when a partial level was taken.
- [What a credential record must hold](atoms/what-a-credential-record-must-hold.md) — a record read as text must parse as JSON and be an object, with api_key and api_secret each a truthy string, so an empty one is rejected while a whitespace-only one is kept and used; every other field is ignored, and the road handed ready-made credentials parses nothing.

- [What the platform's ceilings must be](atoms/what-the-platforms-ceilings-must-be.md) — the ceilings record and the figures record each walk their own fields, every one optional and any supplied one required to be a finite non-negative Decimal, with the utilization field in each held to a further ceiling of one by a test that runs after the walk.
- [The health loop's interval and mark](atoms/the-health-loops-interval-and-mark.md) — the interval must be an int or float and is compared against zero without ever being required to be finite, so a not-a-number interval is stored; starting is ignored while it runs, and the running flag is read three times per tick: at entry, again before anything is written, and again before the next countdown.
- [The turn that ignores the mark](atoms/the-turn-that-ignores-the-mark.md) — a hand-driven turn skips the running-flag guard and schedules nothing, so a stopped loop still reads and applies; where a mode controller is wired the reading is arbitrated against holds and risk rather than written, and the comparison at the end suppresses only the transition log.
- [How a settings field is known to want a tuple](atoms/how-a-settings-field-is-known-to-want-a-tuple.md) — a field is classed as wanting a tuple when its annotation is a tuple or a union containing one, with Annotated wrappers stripped first and no other generic walked into, so a list-of-tuples field keeps its list; the classification runs over both settings registries at import, before any payload exists.
- [What the capital record refuses](atoms/what-the-capital-record-refuses.md) — the pool must be finite and above zero, five further figures finite and zero or more, and the per-strategy mapping must be a mapping whose keys are non-blank strings and whose values are finite non-negative Decimals, the mapping being rebuilt and written back over the field it arrived in.
- [The key this record will not trim for you](atoms/the-key-this-record-will-not-trim-for-you.md) — a strategy id with surrounding whitespace is rejected by the capital record and by the strategy declaration, stripped and kept by the strategy base and the router, and carried as supplied by a command crossing the seam, so which record a padded name meets first decides its fate.
- [What a recovery takes before it rebuilds](atoms/what-a-recovery-takes-before-it-rebuilds.md) — the WAL's magic bytes are checked before anything is read on either road into it, the next sequence number continues from the last record the log still holds rather than the last one written, and the rolling-loss windows are measured back from the clock as recovery runs.
- [What a recovery inserts for a strategy the state forgot](atoms/what-a-recovery-inserts-for-a-strategy-the-state-forgot.md) — a crash can leave the log holding a line about a strategy the saved state has never heard of, so a recovery puts a blank entry in for it before the sums are redone; without that, the pass that redoes them walks only what the saved state already named and the strategy's first result is lost.
- [What a refresh of the loss windows reads](atoms/what-a-refresh-of-the-loss-windows-reads.md) — the periodic re-reckoning of recent losses reads the log itself rather than being given anything, times its windows from the clock at the moment it runs, and zeroes a strategy whose losses have all aged out.
- [What a recovery writes at the end](atoms/what-a-recovery-writes-at-the-end.md) — recovery finishes by setting the cumulative realized figure from the risk record's own per-strategy total, which recomputes the current drawdown while the high-water mark and the worst-ever drawdown are each kept at whichever is larger, restored or recomputed.
- [The keys that must match what they hold](atoms/the-keys-that-must-match-what-they-hold.md) — the positions and per-strategy-mode mappings each reject a value of the wrong type and a key that disagrees with the identifier inside its value, while the dust mapping and both processed-id sets are checked for shape alone and the remaining fields arrive already self-checked.
- [What a tracked order must be](atoms/what-a-tracked-order-must-be.md) — its three identifiers must be strings with content after stripping, though it stores them unstripped; its three amounts must be finite non-negative Decimals with the unfilled part never exceeding the whole; its state must be either sent-and-unacknowledged or acknowledged-and-resting; and its timestamp must carry the UTC marker itself.
- [Starting and stopping the outcome loop](atoms/starting-and-stopping-the-outcome-loop.md) — starting returns at once while a live worker is held and otherwise clears the stop flag and spawns one, stopping takes the worker under the lock, sets the flag, releases the lock and then waits five seconds unless told otherwise, and stopping a loop holding no worker returns before the flag is even set.
- [The stop that blocks a restart](atoms/the-stop-that-blocks-a-restart.md) — a worker still alive when the join expires keeps its slot, which blocks a restart for exactly as long as it runs; the slot is cleared only if it still holds that same worker, while the single shared stop flag means a stop entering after a restart signals the new worker instead.
- [What the host's configuration record refuses](atoms/what-the-hosts-configuration-record-refuses.md) — each venue URL is tested for truthiness alone so a whitespace one is kept, each timeout is compared against zero with no type check so a NaN passes while a string raises from the comparison itself, and the credentials mapping's keys along with the keys and secrets inside its values are all truthiness-tested.
- [What the configuration record holds afterwards](atoms/what-the-configuration-record-holds-afterwards.md) — the record is frozen, so its own construction writes through a call that bypasses that in order to seal copies of its mappings and freeze its mode set; the seal stops a caller mutating the mapping it handed in, while that same bypass stays open for replacing a field entirely.
- [The two readings of the log](atoms/the-two-readings-of-the-log.md) — both walk the WAL from the start and both treat a missing file, an unreadably short header and a torn tail alike, but a header that is present and wrong raises on the strict read and reads as no records on the tolerant one, as do an over-cap length and a CRC mismatch.
- [The check a boot makes before reading](atoms/the-check-a-boot-makes-before-reading.md) — a separate check raises when the file is large enough to hold the magic bytes and does not hold them; it runs as the store is built and again before each tolerant read, while the strict read tests the header itself, and without it a wrong header would surface only at the first write.
- [What the risk records refuse](atoms/what-the-risk-records-refuse.md) — each record sorts its figures in two: high-water marks, drawdowns and the starting capital must be finite and non-negative, while realized and unrealized P&L and the account's equity need only be finite, and the per-strategy mapping rejects a key disagreeing with the id inside its value.
- [The figures the account does not store](atoms/the-figures-the-account-does-not-store.md) — the day, week and month rolling-loss totals and the realized total are summed across the per-strategy records on each read rather than held, each reading one field so a change moves one total, and the sums shift when a strategy is added or dropped with no record having changed.
- [The worst fall a replay reports](atoms/the-worst-fall-a-replay-reports.md) — the peak is seeded from the series' own first value rather than from the stake, so a run starting below its stake reports nothing of that; the worst drop is kept as a fraction of the running peak, a peak at or below zero is skipped instead of divided by, and an empty series answers zero.
- [What the replay report counts a trade as](atoms/what-the-replay-report-counts-a-trade-as.md) — every trade is filed as a buy-and-hold one with nothing checked to decide it, and a trade that finished exactly even is left out of both the winners and the losers while still counting in the overall figure.
- [How the replay report places a fill in a bar](atoms/how-the-replay-report-places-a-fill-in-a-bar.md) — a fill is assigned to a bar by looking up its time among the bars' end times and stepping back, never below the earliest, so a fill from before the first bar is moved into it and nothing records the move.
- [When the risk-adjusted figure is absent](atoms/when-the-risk-adjusted-figure-is-absent.md) — it is left empty for a non-positive interval, for fewer than two returns, and for a zero variance; a step whose previous equity is at or below zero forms no return at all, which can starve the count, and otherwise the answer is the mean over the standard deviation, annualised by a square root.
- [How a call across the seam is made](atoms/how-a-call-across-the-seam-is-made.md) — each call is refused outright if its function was never wired, four are bridged onto Praxis's event loop and awaited for one shared span after which the pending call is asked to stop, without that asking being waited for or checked, and registering an account and pulling its positions cross directly with no span at all.
- [The two ways the signed figures change](atoms/the-two-ways-the-signed-figures-change.md) — each signed P&L figure has a setter that rejects a non-finite value and recomputes the drawdowns, but the record is a plain mutable dataclass, so a restore from the log writes both fields directly and leaves the drawdowns answering to the last setter call instead.
- [The five re-keyings](atoms/the-five-re-keyings.md) — each cross-seam enum field goes through one shared re-keying that is handed the field name, so a rejection says which field it concerned; a missing maker preference and a missing self-trade setting are answered before that call, and one field also carries a table of equivalent words.
- [The builder that stands apart](atoms/the-builder-that-stands-apart.md) — the single-order mode's parameters go to their own builder before the per-mode table is consulted, both roads hand back an already-correct object unexamined, and where a mapping arrives the single-order builder checks each value itself while the table's road leaves that to the record it fills.
- [What a trade is on the portfolio basis](atoms/what-a-trade-is-on-the-portfolio-basis.md) — a trade is a run of consecutive in-position steps, closed by the first step out and recorded as it stands if the steps end first, with its returns compounded and reported as the run's gain rather than a sum, where the bar basis instead numbers bars by a running count of entry marks.
- [How the portfolio windows are cut](atoms/how-the-portfolio-windows-are-cut.md) — steps are grouped by truncating each timestamp to a caller-named span, a day by default, where the bar basis offers no such choice; each window reports its compounded return and that return over the fraction of steps in position, which is absent when no step held one.
- [What the depth poller is built with](atoms/what-the-depth-poller-is-built-with.md) — its poll interval and request span are compared against zero, its URL and token tested for truthiness alone, its depth floor asked only for finiteness and positivity with no type check, and its stuck-poll threshold required to be at least two while a not-a-number value passes and disables the check it feeds.
- [Starting and stopping the depth poller](atoms/starting-and-stopping-the-depth-poller.md) — starting raises while it already runs and otherwise clears the flag, opens a session and spawns the loop; stopping sets the flag and awaits the task, suppressing only the error a cancelled task raises, so another failure escapes with the task still held and the session still open.
- [What binsim's read endpoints answer](atoms/what-binsims-read-endpoints-answer.md) — health and time ignore the request entirely, the trading-rules endpoint checks a symbol only when one is supplied, and the order-book endpoint insists on one, reporting a missing symbol under a different code from an unrecognised one.
- [What binsim's server holds and when](atoms/what-binsims-server-holds-and-when.md) — it is built with its web application already made, and takes hold of the pieces that serve requests only once a start has succeeded, so a failed start leaves it holding neither of them and reading as stopped.
- [The two sockets binsim accepts](atoms/the-two-sockets-binsim-accepts.md) — one connection reads what arrives and replies, the other reads what arrives and throws it away, and both stay up on the same keepalive, so a caller cannot tell a discarded message from one that was acted on.
- [What a submitted order is weighed against](atoms/what-a-submitted-order-is-weighed-against.md) — the book's staleness is measured from the last successful price fetch rather than from the last time the book changed, and the lot rules are applied after the book walk on one sizing road and before it on the other.
- [What a fill's fee is charged in](atoms/what-a-fills-fee-is-charged-in.md) — at every price level a buy is charged in coin and a sale in cash, so a round trip is charged in two different assets and its fees cannot be added together, and an account with no balance record is told its order was rejected rather than its key refused.
- [What a subscription frame is answered with](atoms/what-a-subscription-frame-is-answered-with.md) — a message the simulator cannot parse is dropped without reply while anything it can parse always gets one, and a value sent as the wrong type reaches the emptiness test as empty.
- [What the application is handed and what it serves](atoms/what-the-application-is-handed-and-what-it-serves.md) — the book, the balances, the price feed and the staleness limit are each attached to the application under their own key, every handler reaching them through it, and one path answers about an order or submits one depending on the method used.
- [What an order's figures are put through](atoms/what-an-orders-figures-are-put-through.md) — rounding an amount only ever lowers it, and the smallest-value check returns immediately when the book has no price to measure against, so such an order passes it unmeasured.
- [What asking for a signature buys](atoms/what-asking-for-a-signature-buys.md) — the endpoints that demand a signed caller use it for the account name, and an account the simulator has never heard of comes back as a refused signature, so a bad key and an unknown account look identical.
- [The two shapes a refusal takes](atoms/the-two-shapes-a-refusal-takes.md) — the routine that builds an error returns one to be thrown for two known statuses and throws one itself for anything else, so the same call is a factory on one path and a failure on the other.
- [What the feed demands of a depth payload](atoms/what-the-feed-demands-of-a-depth-payload.md) — the reply must be an object and so must the part read from it, while the timestamp, the book version and each side of the book are looked up by key, so a missing field is reported by name and a wrong shape by what arrived.
- [What the feed keeps and what it waits on](atoms/what-the-feed-keeps-and-what-it-waits-on.md) — it holds a stop signal and three tallies that all start at zero, and between requests it waits on that signal with the interval as a ceiling, so a stop lands at once rather than after the current interval.
- [The lock the marking insists on](atoms/the-lock-the-marking-insists-on.md) — where a positions lock is supplied it must be the same object the risk record holds, or construction is refused; a record holding none fails the same test, while a loop given no lock is built without the question being asked at all.
- [The mark that ends a turn](atoms/the-mark-that-ends-a-turn.md) — symbols are read inside whatever guard the loop was given and the marks fetched outside it, and a mark that is missing or not a finite Decimal ends the entire turn at that point, leaving every position's unrealized figure as it stood and the later symbols unasked.
- [How the mark loop paces itself](atoms/how-the-mark-loop-paces-itself.md) — each valuation round is scheduled by a fresh background timer made only after the previous round has finished, so the gap runs end to end and two rounds can never overlap; a stop part-way through lets the round in progress finish and schedules no other.
- [What a mark works out for each position](atoms/what-a-mark-works-out-for-each-position.md) — a holding's paper profit is the current price less the price paid, times the amount, with the direction of the trade deciding the sign; one walk builds the figure for each holding, each strategy and the whole, and nothing is written until all three are done.
- [What the mark loop leaves unguarded](atoms/what-the-mark-loop-leaves-unguarded.md) — the flag saying the round is on is read without the lock every write of it takes, a round can be asked for directly on a loop that was never started, and a round that throws is written to the log while the next is scheduled as usual.
- [What each action is submitted inside](atoms/what-each-action-is-submitted-inside.md) — every action is handled inside a bound context carrying the strategy id and action type, plus trade and command ids where present but nothing naming the action itself; an abort forks away first, and a context builder raising an ordinary failure costs that action alone.
- [When a submission does not reach capital](atoms/when-a-submission-does-not-reach-capital.md) — converting a submission into a tracked order is skipped for a non-submitted status, a missing command id, or no reservation, which is the ordinary course for aborts, exits and amendments; but the reservation is taken before the send, so a skip never means the account was left alone.
- [What an abort and a modify answer with](atoms/what-an-abort-and-a-modify-answer-with.md) — an instruction that goes out without a command being built for it comes back refused, failed on the way out, or sent; a request to change an order already placed is refused for any of several missing pieces, where a request to stop one is refused only for a missing name.
- [How the walk over a strategy's actions ends each one](atoms/how-the-walk-over-a-strategys-actions-ends-each-one.md) — each instruction a strategy returns is dealt with in turn and leaves exactly one reply in a growing list, every route carrying its own reply rather than meeting at a common ending, and nothing is given back until the last one is done.
- [What the ledger refuses to apply](atoms/what-the-ledger-refuses-to-apply.md) — an event for another account is rejected before the lock is even taken, then inside it a registration is handled first, anything arriving before one is rejected, a repeat registration is rejected, and only past those does a fill meet the symbol test; a close for an unknown trade is silently ignored.
- [What a rebuilt ledger carries](atoms/what-a-rebuilt-ledger-carries.md) — the resumable state holds balances, per-trade realized and fee figures, and open lots, but no journal, so the posting history behind each figure is gone; lots return in the order the snapshot listed them, and under average costing there is one per trade to return.
- [How a journal entry moves the balances](atoms/how-a-journal-entry-moves-the-balances.md) — the entry is written to the journal before any balance moves, and each of its lines shifts an account in the direction that account itself dictates, so the same pair of figures moves two accounts opposite ways.
- [What the ledger refuses](atoms/what-the-ledger-refuses.md) — it will not be built without an account name, treats a fill in any other coin as something it was never built to do, and throws on a sale larger than the holdings it has open, naming the shortfall.
- [What a reading of the ledger hands back](atoms/what-a-reading-of-the-ledger-hands-back.md) — every way of reading it takes its lock and returns freshly made values, copying each per-trade record rather than just the mapping, so what a reader keeps never shifts underneath them.
- [The figures a fill is assumed to carry](atoms/the-figures-a-fill-is-assumed-to-carry.md) — the fill size, price, notional and fees are checked by assertion rather than by a test that could answer, the record's own validation having already required all four; the capital call runs before any position is read, so a failing call returns a failure while a missing trade or position raises later.
- [The order the intake hooks stand in](atoms/the-order-the-intake-hooks-stand-in.md) — a mode check runs before any hook, then the duplicate-window hook is built first and the reference hook second, with a resolved rate cap inserted ahead of both; each rejects a non-integer or non-positive figure at build time, and each ignores anything but an entry.
- [What the intake stage holds its hooks to](atoms/what-the-intake-stage-holds-its-hooks-to.md) — the first stage of checking throws an error rather than turning a trade away when a check it runs replies with the wrong sort of thing, and the stage's own approval is its own, not any check's.
- [What an intake hook keeps between calls](atoms/what-an-intake-hook-keeps-between-calls.md) — the checks that count events over a period build their tally and its lock once and share them across every call, read the clock a single time per call, and can be handed a clock of their own.
- [What the reference check asks of a change or a stop](atoms/what-the-reference-check-asks-of-a-change-or-a-stop.md) — a request to change an order is matched against a list that can be fetched fresh, while a request to stop one is matched against a list fixed when the check was built, so an order that became stoppable since then is unknown to one and known to the other.
- [What intake asks of an exit](atoms/what-intake-asks-of-an-exit.md) — an exit must be a SELL, name a trade with an open position, own that position, give a size above zero, and keep that size within what remains after already-pending exits, each asked only once the previous one passes.
- [What a loop's stop leaves running](atoms/what-a-loops-stop-leaves-running.md) — switching off a repeating background job cancels its next run but lets the run already going finish, so a write can still land after the switch-off has returned.
- [What the halted mode turns away](atoms/what-the-halted-mode-turns-away.md) — while the system is coming down, a strategy's request to open, close or alter is refused by the usual checks, yet two kinds of order still go out, one of them closing positions by a route that skips those checks.
- [Stopping the loop that reads outcomes](atoms/stopping-the-loop-that-reads-outcomes.md) — the reader being switched off is asked to finish and given a while, and one still going when the while is up is left to complete the turn it is in, which can take one more message away from whoever reads next.
- [What a strategy is handed when it is asked to stop](atoms/what-a-strategy-is-handed-when-it-is-asked-to-stop.md) — the holdings it owns, the money it still has room for, and the state trading is in, with its own settings arriving empty.
- [The abort a host sends as it stops](atoms/the-abort-a-host-sends-as-it-stops.md) — a request to drop an order already handed over, naming it by a label the strategy itself supplies.
- [What a stopping host waits for](atoms/what-a-stopping-host-waits-for.md) — it keeps a list as it sends and reads replies until that list is clear; should a set time pass with entries left, it tries to drop them and reads once more for half as long before going down regardless.
- [What a sequencer refuses to be built with](atoms/what-a-sequencer-refuses-to-be-built-with.md) — building the thing that brings a host down insists on five pieces being present and of the right sort, allows the settings to be absent, and refuses only one combination: the account named twice and named differently.
- [What the wait for outcomes takes off the pending set](atoms/what-the-wait-for-outcomes-takes-off-the-pending-set.md) — the host waits for answers on what it still owes, checks the clock before taking each answer rather than after acting on one, and strikes a command off only when an answer ends it, so a partly filled one keeps the wait open.
- [The same absence in the two exit builders](atoms/the-same-absence-in-the-two-exit-builders.md) — a closing instruction with no trade named is quietly skipped by the first builder and throws in the second, so the first decides which holdings are even attempted and the second trusts what it is handed.
- [What each shutdown submission records](atoms/what-each-shutdown-submission-records.md) — with no way out or no settings, the sending does nothing and says nothing while the giving up says so; a sent closing raises the holding's reserved-for-sale amount and is filed under the name the far side gave back.
- [Where an outcome outside the wait is sent](atoms/where-an-outcome-outside-the-wait-is-sent.md) — a reply arrives for an order the shutdown is not watching, and a second route hands it to the code that keeps the books, which records it against the order where it has a file on one, and otherwise asks for the money back if the reply is a final one.
- [The name an order comes back with](atoms/the-name-an-order-comes-back-with.md) — the label the other side answers with when an order is handed over, and when a label that differs from what was asked for is treated as a failure.
- [Which name a command is kept under](atoms/which-name-a-command-is-kept-under.md) — an order is filed under either the label the sender chose or the label the other side answered with, and which of those applies was fixed long before the order went.
- [Claiming the money after the send](atoms/when-money-set-aside-moves-onto-an-order.md) — on one route the money held in reserve is re-booked against an order only once that order has gone, so a refusal arrives too late to stop it and can leave the reply with no strategy to attach it to.
- [What a strategy’s committed figure moves on](atoms/what-moves-a-strategys-committed-figure.md) — the money held against one strategy is raised on purpose only where it is set aside for a trade; the rest of what touches it is giving money back, except a settling fee that came in over its guess, which pushes it up in the ordinary way of things; a startup replaces it outright.
- [What a pending sale refuses](atoms/what-a-pending-sale-refuses.md) — the cap on selling the same holding twice is applied by the first check a request meets, and the sales a host fires off as it shuts down never meet it.
- [What a pending sale does to the dust](atoms/what-a-pending-sale-does-to-the-dust.md) — an unsettled sale bars one route to writing a holding off and leaves the other wide open, so the same leftover is treated two ways depending on how it got there.
- [What a pending sale is](atoms/what-a-pending-sale-is.md) — a holding records how much of it already has a sale out, which caps what a further sale asking through the checks may request.
- [What a written-back position carries](atoms/what-a-written-back-position-carries.md) — a holding rebuilt at startup from the venue side takes one price as both the price it opened at and the price its money is booked at, where a holding built in the ordinary way keeps those apart.
- [What the host counts when a fill empties a position](atoms/what-the-host-counts-when-a-fill-empties-a-position.md) — a holding is gone before the message announcing its close arrives, so a tally stands in for it and tells a sold-out trade from one that never held anything.
- [Where a position's last scraps go](atoms/where-a-positions-last-scraps-go.md) — the part of a holding too small to sell is written off to a running total per coin, and no decision ever looks at that total again.
- [What a dust close refuses before it touches anything](atoms/what-a-dust-close-refuses-before-it-touches-anything.md) — writing off a tiny holding is given three names and each must be real text, all three checked before any part of the book is held, so a badly asked write-off blocks nothing.
- [What a taking-out takes](atoms/what-a-taking-out-takes.md) — releasing the money behind a sale removes what was asked for or as much as is there, and a strategy left with a sliver is dropped from the books entirely.
- [What a start weighs a position at](atoms/what-a-start-weighs-a-position-at.md) — startup values each holding at a different price depending on where it came from, and writes the price it settled on back onto the holding.
- [What a start does with a dusted position](atoms/what-a-start-does-with-a-dusted-position.md) — startup asks the venue side what it is holding and writes back anything missing, so a holding written off can reappear with its money attached.
- [What a dusted scrap leaves behind](atoms/what-a-dusted-scrap-leaves-behind.md) — writing off a holding too small to sell leaves the money it was bought with counted against nothing until the next startup.
- [What money arriving in the account changes](atoms/what-money-arriving-in-the-account-changes.md) — paying money in is written to the log and buys nothing: the sum every allowance is a slice of is fixed when the host starts and never moves.
- [What a fee figure is worked out from](atoms/what-a-fee-figure-is-worked-out-from.md) — the trading cost in a report is a flat thousandth of the fill, while the ledger alongside it books what the venue actually charged, so each trade is recorded as costing one amount in one place and another in the other.
- [Which fills come back with a result](atoms/which-fills-come-back-with-a-result.md) — a purchase reports no gain or loss at all, and the gain on a sale is measured from the price the holding was opened at rather than the price its money is booked at.
- [Where a realized result is written down](atoms/where-a-realized-result-is-written-down.md) — what a sale made or lost is saved to the record before it is counted, so a save that fails leaves nothing counted that was never saved.
- [Where the locks sit on a position change](atoms/where-the-locks-sit-on-a-position-change.md) — the sums are worked out before the holdings are held and only the writing happens inside the hold, except for one routine that holds them before it even looks, and one way in that nothing calls.
- [What the deployed figures out of step refuse](atoms/what-the-deployed-figures-out-of-step-refuse.md) — two records of committed money are kept, and while they disagree no new holding can be opened, though sales and cancellations go through untouched.
- [What a boot rebuilds from the positions](atoms/what-a-boot-rebuilds-from-the-positions.md) — startup works out each strategy’s committed money again from the holdings it can see, and without that pass the first order of the new run is turned down and so is every one after it.
- [What a boot finds stranded](atoms/what-a-boot-finds-stranded.md) — money the last run had committed with nothing left to give it back is written off at startup, at the risk of committing it twice.
- [What the fee reserve absorbs](atoms/what-the-fee-reserve-absorbs.md) — trading costs are guessed before a trade and billed after it, and the account holds money back for the gap.
- [What an exit outcome changes](atoms/what-an-exit-outcome-changes.md) — closing a holding gives money back and reports what the trade made, and those are two different sums.
- [When an entry's position grows](atoms/when-an-entrys-position-grows.md) — buying in pieces leaves one holding at an averaged price, and what that average is later measured against.
- [An outcome waiting on a strategy](atoms/an-outcome-waiting-on-a-strategy.md) — a report matching no strategy yet is set aside, everything after it for that command waits behind, and a wait that never ends costs them all.

## How this wiki works

- [The register](register.md) — every topic, whether it is written yet, and everything found while reading the code.
- [The file ledger](ledger.md) — which source files the wiki accounts for, and which it has not touched.
- [Which commits it describes](BASELINE.md) — every article is written against one version of the code, and names it.
- [The article template](TEMPLATE.md) — what every page must carry, including the word limit.

Each article has a word limit. One that outgrows it is split in two rather than
made longer, which is why some titles are narrow.

Both sides are the subject, and Praxis is the side that has been written. 524
articles exist. Of Praxis's own source files 81 are accounted for in full and 7 in
part, with none left untouched; and all 70 in Nexus are named, none
untouched — 64 carry a row of their own, and 6 are reached by an article, a
recorded finding, or a recorded exclusion, all six recorded as shapes with no
behaviour to trace. Depth is the work that remains: of the 64 Nexus modules
carrying a row, 63 are accounted for in full and 1 in part. [The file ledger](ledger.md) names every file on both sides, so a
subject with no article can be found there rather than guessed at.
